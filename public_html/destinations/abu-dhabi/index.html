GOOD TO KNOW

Visiting Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi, the UAE's capital, is about 90 minutes' drive from Dubai and works as a day trip or a stay of its own. Yas Island has three big theme parks close together: Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World and Yas Waterworld. Saadiyat Island is the culture district, with Louvre Abu Dhabi. Qasr Al Watan, the presidential palace, and the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque show the city's heritage; dress modestly for both. A private transfer from Dubai or a city tour with pickup is the easiest way to fit several sights into one day.
