FAQ

Holiday package questions

What is included in a KWIKAR holiday package?

A package is built from a hotel stay, the activities and tours you choose and private transfers, for example from the airport to your hotel. Your quote lists exactly what is included. Anything not listed in the quote, such as flights, is not part of the package.

How much does a Dubai holiday package cost?

The price depends on your travel dates, the hotel you choose, the number of nights and the ages of your guests, so we do not show fixed package prices. Send your plans on WhatsApp and our team replies with a quote in AED. The confirmed quote is the total, with no separate card or booking fee.

Can I change the hotel, nights or activities?

Yes. The packages on this page are starting ideas. Tell us which hotel area, how many nights and which activities you want to add or remove, and our team sends an updated quote before you pay anything.

How do I book a holiday package?

Choose a package and tap Request a quote, or message us on WhatsApp with your dates, number of guests and budget. There is no online payment. You pay only after our team has confirmed the hotel, activities, transfers and final price.

Can you help with a UAE tourist visa for my holiday?

Yes. We offer UAE tourist visa assistance for 14, 30 or 60 days, which you can add to your Dubai or Abu Dhabi holiday. Visa approval is decided by the issuing authority, not by KWIKAR.

Can you plan a holiday that is not listed here?

Yes. Send us the destination, dates and what you would like to do, and our team checks what can be arranged and sends a tailored quote.

What if I need to cancel my holiday?

Holiday packages can have different cancellation terms depending on the hotel and activities. We share the terms that apply with your quote, before we ask for payment, so you can check them first.
