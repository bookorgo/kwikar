Overview

Global Village is a seasonal park in Dubai that opens in the cooler months. Season 31 has 29 cultural pavilions representing more than 90 cultures, with over 3,500 shopping outlets and more than 200 attractions, from food stalls and craft shops to stage shows and rides.

Each pavilion is themed on a country or region, with its own façade, food and goods for sale. Between the pavilions you will find live performances, stunt shows and family rides. New this season are the Fast Live 2 stunt show, the Bollywood Idol and Bank Heist shows, a Season 31 Passport you can stamp at every pavilion, and a Kids Passport. A new roller coaster, Tokyo Rush, is due to open later in the season.

Your KWIKAR ticket covers entry to Global Village. Food, shopping and anything you buy inside are paid on the day. See more theme parks in the UAE or family activities.

Best for

  • Families: Rides, shows and a Kids Passport to stamp at each pavilion.
  • Food lovers: Street food and restaurants from dozens of countries in one place.
  • Souvenir shopping: Crafts, spices, textiles and gifts from the pavilions.
  • An evening out: It opens at 4pm and stays open late, so it fits after a day of sightseeing.
KWIKAR tip

Weekend evenings are the busiest. Arrive soon after 4pm on a weekday for shorter queues, and remember that Tuesday is for families, ladies and couples.

About this experience

How much are Global Village tickets?

On KWIKAR, Global Village entry starts from AED 30 per person. Our team confirms the final price for your date on WhatsApp before you pay.

What are Global Village opening hours?

Global Village opens at 4pm every day. It closes at midnight from Sunday to Wednesday, and at 1am from Thursday to Saturday and on public holidays.

When does Season 31 start?

Season 31 opened on Wednesday 14 October 2026.

What time is last entry?

The entry gates close half an hour before Global Village closes: 11:30pm from Sunday to Wednesday and 12:30am from Thursday to Saturday.

Is Tuesday a family day at Global Village?

Yes. Tuesday is reserved for families, ladies and couples, except on public holidays.

Which days can I use the ticket?

This is an any-day entrance ticket, so it works on every day of the week. Tell us your date when you book.

How many pavilions are there?

Season 31 has 29 cultural pavilions representing more than 90 cultures, plus over 3,500 shopping outlets.

What is new in Season 31?

New this season are the Fast Live 2 stunt show, the Bollywood Idol and Bank Heist shows, a Season 31 Passport and a Kids Passport. The Tokyo Rush roller coaster is due to open later in the season.

Can I cancel my Global Village ticket?

No. Global Village tickets are non-refundable once issued, so check your date before you pay.

Is hotel pickup included?

No, the ticket is entry only. We can add a private transfer from your hotel at extra cost; ask our team on WhatsApp.
