It is open every day: Monday to Thursday 10am–11pm, Friday 10am–midnight, and Saturday and Sunday 9am–midnight. Overview

Ski Dubai is an indoor ski resort inside Mall of the Emirates, with a real snow slope, chairlifts and a family Snow Park. Ski Dubai says the snow is kept at about -4°C, so a visit is a quick way to swap the desert heat for winter on any day of the year.

Both passes we offer are built around the Snow Park. It has a Snow Cavern, tobogganing, a giant Zorb ball, a bobsleigh run, a tube run and bumper cars, and your pass covers the whole day. Winter clothing is part of the ticket: a jacket, trousers, disposable socks, snow boots and a pair of fleece gloves. Helmets are compulsory for children under 13.

Snow Classic is the simple option: unlimited Snow Park access and one chairlift ride over the slope. Snow Plus adds unlimited chairlift and Mountain Thriller rides, a locker, and one extra experience: either two hours on the ski slope or two rides on the Snow Bullet zipline. The slope session is only for skiers and snowboarders who are already at intermediate level 2; beginners should pick the zipline.

Ski Dubai is an easy half day to pair with other indoor sights, such as the Dubai Aquarium or Burj Khalifa At the Top. Our team checks your request and confirms the final price before you pay.

Best for

  • First time in snow: Sledging, tubing and snow play with no skiing needed.
  • Families: Rides for all ages from 2, with winter clothing included.
  • Hot days: An indoor winter break in any month of the year.
  • Skiers: Snow Plus includes a 2-hour slope session for intermediate level 2 and above.
KWIKAR tip

Weekday mornings just after opening are usually the quietest. If you choose Snow Plus with the slope session, sign Ski Dubai's online waiver before you arrive to save time at the counter.

Visitor information

Opening hours
Monday to Thursday 10am–11pm (last ticket 9:30pm), Friday 10am–midnight (last ticket 10:30pm), Saturday and Sunday 9am–midnight (last ticket 10:30pm). Hours can change on public holidays; our team confirms the time for your date.
Children
Children under 2 are not allowed inside. Guests under 14 must be with an adult aged 16 or over, and children under 13 must wear a helmet. Rides have their own height, weight and age limits. Send everyone's ages and our team confirms the right tickets.
The ski slope
The 2-hour slope session with Snow Plus is only for skiers and snowboarders at intermediate level 2 or above, and children under 3 are not allowed on the slope. Every slope user must sign Ski Dubai's waiver online before the visit, and a parent or guardian signs for guests under 21.
What to wear
Jacket, trousers, boots, socks and gloves are provided. A warm layer underneath and a woolly hat make the cold more comfortable; it is about -4°C inside.
Getting there
Ski Dubai is inside Mall of the Emirates in Al Barsha. Take the Metro Red Line to Mall of the Emirates station, or ask us to add a private transfer.
Food
Outside food and drinks are not allowed. There are cafés inside the snow park and restaurants in the mall.

About this experience

How much are Ski Dubai tickets?

Snow Classic starts from AED 225 per person, and the live price of both passes is shown on this page. Our team confirms the final price on WhatsApp before you pay.

What are the Ski Dubai timings?

Monday to Thursday 10am–11pm, Friday 10am–midnight, and Saturday and Sunday 9am–midnight. The last ticket is sold at 9:30pm Monday to Thursday and 10:30pm Friday to Sunday.

Where is Ski Dubai?

Inside Mall of the Emirates in Al Barsha, Dubai. The nearest Metro stop is Mall of the Emirates on the Red Line.

What is the difference between Snow Classic and Snow Plus?

Both give full-day Snow Park access and winter clothing. Snow Classic includes one chairlift ride. Snow Plus adds unlimited chairlift and Mountain Thriller rides, a locker, and either a 2-hour slope session or 2 Snow Bullet zipline rides.

Do I need to know how to ski?

Not for the Snow Park, sledging or the zipline. The slope session in Snow Plus is only for skiers and snowboarders at intermediate level 2 or above, so beginners should choose the Snow Bullet rides instead.

What should I wear to Ski Dubai?

Jacket, trousers, disposable socks, snow boots and fleece gloves are included. Wear comfortable clothes underneath and bring a woolly hat, as it is about -4°C inside.

Can children go to Ski Dubai?

Yes, from age 2. Children under 2 are not allowed in, guests under 14 must be with an adult aged 16 or over, and children under 13 must wear a helmet. Some rides have their own height and age limits.

Is the penguin encounter included?

No. The penguin encounter and ski or snowboard lessons are not part of these two passes. Ask our team on WhatsApp if you would like one.

Can I bring food into Ski Dubai?

No, outside food and drinks are not allowed. There are cafés inside the snow park for hot drinks and snacks.

How long should I plan for?

Your pass covers the whole day, but most families are happy with two to four hours in the snow. Allow extra time with Snow Plus for the slope session or zipline.
