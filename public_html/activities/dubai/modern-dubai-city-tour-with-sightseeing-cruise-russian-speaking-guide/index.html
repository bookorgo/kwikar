Overview

Dubai is a city of the future, born from the desert in just a few decades — yet its glass towers and futuristic skyline still make room for Eastern charm and tradition.

This tour reveals Dubai's many faces: bold and ambitious by day, glowing with light by night, where ultramodern architecture and old Arabian atmosphere exist side by side.

What awaits you

  • Dubai Marina: a scenic boat cruise along the canal, past a wall of skyscrapers and yacht-lined docks.
  • Palm Jumeirah: a monorail ride over the man-made island, with views of the Atlantis Hotel, waterfront villas and the Persian Gulf.
  • Burj Al Arab: a photo stop at the sail-shaped hotel, the emirate's most iconic landmark.
  • Madinat Jumeirah: canals, wooden bridges and a covered souk in the style of an old Arabian town.
  • The Dubai Mall & singing fountains: a giant aquarium, an evening fountain show and views of the Burj Khalifa.

Perfect for

  • First-time visitors: all of Dubai's main landmarks in a single day, without planning your own route.
  • Families with children: a comfortable air-conditioned bus and an easy pace suited to all ages.
  • Photography enthusiasts: from the skyline of the Marina to the canals of Madinat Jumeirah and the illuminated fountains at night.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

Wear smart casual, comfortable clothing — you'll be on your feet for much of the day. Bring something warm, as the bus is air-conditioned.

Is a return transfer included?

Yes, the return transfer is included in the price. If you decide to stay longer at The Dubai Mall or leave earlier, please let your guide know in advance.

What are the conditions for children?

Children under 3 travel free of charge, without a separate seat. Children aged 3 to 11 pay a child ticket, and from 12 years the adult ticket applies. For the Burj Khalifa observation deck: children under 3 are free, ages 3 to 7 inclusive pay a child ticket, and from 8 years the adult ticket applies.
