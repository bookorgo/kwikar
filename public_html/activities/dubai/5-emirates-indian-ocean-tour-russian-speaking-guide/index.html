Overview

The United Arab Emirates is far more than Dubai's skyline — five emirates apart, each with its own character, unfold within a single day's drive: the cultural heart of Sharjah, the quiet traditions of Ajman and Umm Al Quwain, the rugged mountains of Ras Al Khaimah and the warm shores of the Indian Ocean in Fujairah.

This is a day of contrasts — from a centuries-old mosque and a quiet mangrove coastline to desert dunes rising into rocky peaks, and finally the gentle waves of the East Coast. By evening, you'll have felt the country's full range, far beyond its famous glass towers.

What awaits you

  • Sharjah: a photo stop at the King Faisal Mosque and its landmark square.
  • Ajman: a visit to the Ajman National Museum, housed in an 18th-century fort.
  • Umm Al Quwain: a drive past the mangrove forests of the UAE's quietest emirate.
  • Ras Al Khaimah: a scenic mountain drive from desert dunes to rugged peaks and coastal views.
  • Fujairah & the Indian Ocean: a visit to Al Bidya, the UAE's oldest mosque, followed by free time to swim on the East Coast.

Perfect for

  • First-time visitors: five emirates and the East Coast covered in one comprehensive day trip.
  • Beach and nature lovers: mangrove forests, mountain scenery and a swim in the Indian Ocean.
  • Culture and history enthusiasts: mosques, a national museum and the country's oldest fort in a single itinerary.

What is the dress code?

Smart casual, modest clothing is recommended; closed clothing is required for the museum visit. Bring swimwear, a towel, a hat, sunglasses and sunscreen for the beach stop.

Yes, a return transfer to your hotel is included in the price.

Children under 3 travel free of charge, without a separate seat. Children aged 3 to 11 inclusive pay a child ticket, and from 12 years the adult ticket applies.
