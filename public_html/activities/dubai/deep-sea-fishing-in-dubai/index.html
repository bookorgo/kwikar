Overview

Fishing is an integral part of the culture and everyday life of local residents, as the Persian Gulf is rich in various types of fish, such as: barracuda, sea bass, tuna, and cobia. 
Accompanied by an experienced crew, you will embark on a comfortable boat and cruise the waters of the Persian Gulf. Fishing takes place on high-speed boats equipped with all the necessary equipment for bottom fishing, trolling, and spinning. 

Whether you are an experienced angler or a novice, the thrill of the catch and a great mood will always be with you.
