Questions

About this experience

WHAT IS AYA UNIVERSE?

Aya Universe is an immersive entertainment park located in Dubai, offering a unique experience that blends art, technology, and nature. It’s a sanctuary beyond our known universe, where visitors can explore vibrant worlds and engage with the natural beauty of a mysterious cosmos.

WHERE IS AYA UNIVERSE LOCATED?

Aya Universe is located on the first floor of WAFI Mall, Oud Metha Road, Dubai, UAE. It’s easily accessible by car and public transport, with parking options available​.

WHAT ARE THE OPENING HOURS OF AYA UNIVERSE?

Aya Universe is open Sunday to Thursday from 10 AM to 10 PM, and Friday to Saturday from 10 AM to 12 AM. The last entry is allowed one hour before closing​​.

ARE THERE ANY AGE RESTRICTIONS FOR VISITORS?

Aya Universe welcomes visitors of all ages. However, some exhibits may have age or height restrictions for safety purposes. Please check specific exhibit details for more information.

IS AYA UNIVERSE ACCESSIBLE TO PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES?

Yes, Aya Universe is designed to be accessible to all our visitors. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us in advance so we can assist you.

CAN I BRING MY OWN FOOD AND DRINKS TO AYA UNIVERSE?

Visitors are advised to not bring outside food and drinks into Aya Universe. There is a variety of dining options available within the park.

IS PHOTOGRAPHY ALLOWED IN AYA UNIVERSE?

Yes, photography is allowed, and visitors are encouraged to capture their memorable moments. However, commercial photography may require prior permission.
