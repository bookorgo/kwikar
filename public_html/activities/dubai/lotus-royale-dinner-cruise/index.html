Lotus Royale Mega Yacht Dinner Cruise in Dubai Marina

Dubai Marina · Boarding 6:45pm · 3-hour buffet dinner cruise

3-hour mega yacht cruiseBuffet dinner and live entertainmentBook on WhatsApp

Lotus Royale is a large mega yacht that leaves Dubai Marina at 7:30pm for a 3-hour dinner cruise along the coast, with a buffet, soft drinks and live entertainment. The Standard Package starts from AED 299 per person; VIP, unlimited-drinks and Royale packages cost more. Boarding opens at 6:45pm near Pier 7 by Dubai Marina Mall, and late guests count as a no-show.

Overview

Lotus Royale is a mega yacht, much larger than the wooden dhows in Dubai Marina, so the evening feels more like a floating restaurant with several decks to walk around. It sails out of the Marina into the open Gulf and stays out for about three hours, which gives you time for dinner, the show and the views without rushing.

The route leaves Dubai Marina past the towers of JBR and Ain Dubai on Bluewaters Island, then follows the coast towards Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis and the Burj Al Arab. How far the yacht goes depends on the sea and the weather on the night.

Every package includes the buffet dinner, water, soft drinks and live entertainment. The packages differ in seating and drinks: VIP adds premium seating, two packages add unlimited alcoholic drinks, and the Royale Package adds a dance-floor table, lounge access and its own food and drinks. If you prefer a shorter evening inside the Marina, compare the Alexandra dhow dinner cruise or see all Dubai cruises.

Best for

  • A special night out: A longer cruise than a dhow, with open-sea views of the Palm and Burj Al Arab.
  • Groups and celebrations: Plenty of deck space, live entertainment and a dance floor on board.
  • Families: Children under 3 are free and there is a child price on most packages.

Which should I choose?

  • Lowest price: Standard Package: buffet, soft drinks and the show.
  • Better seats: VIP Package adds premium seating, with soft drinks.
  • Drinks included: Standard Package + Unlimited Alcoholic Drinks, or VIP + Unlimited Premium Drinks with premium seating.
  • The full experience: Royale Package: dance-floor table, priority check-in, Royale Lounge access, live cooking stations and bubbly.

About this experience

How much is the Lotus Royale dinner cruise?

On KWIKAR, the Lotus Royale cruise starts from AED 299 per person for the Standard Package. VIP, unlimited-drinks and Royale packages cost more; the current price of every package is in the table above, and our team confirms the final price before you pay.

What time does the Lotus Royale cruise start?

Boarding starts at 6:45pm and the yacht departs at 7:30pm. The cruise lasts about 3 hours.

Where do I board the Lotus Royale?

In Dubai Marina, near Pier 7 next to Dubai Marina Mall. Your confirmation includes the exact berth.

What happens if I am late?

The yacht leaves on time. Guests who miss it are treated as a no-show, without a refund or a new date, so plan to arrive by 7:00pm.

What is the dress code?

Smart casual. In winter, bring a warm layer for the open decks.

What is the child policy?

Children under 3 travel free. From age 3 a child ticket is needed, and older children pay the adult price; send the ages and our team confirms the right ticket. On the VIP soft-drinks package the child price is the same as the adult price.

Can I reserve a specific table?

Not on the Standard packages: seats are first come, first served. VIP packages have premium seating, and the Royale Package includes a dance-floor table.

What is the difference between Standard and VIP?

Both include the 3-hour cruise, buffet dinner, soft drinks and live entertainment. VIP adds premium seating. Each comes with or without unlimited drinks; the VIP drinks package includes premium alcoholic drinks.

Is hotel pickup included?

No. You meet the yacht in Dubai Marina. We can add a private transfer from your hotel on request.

Can I cancel?

Changes and refunds follow the supplier's policy, which allows cancellation 24 hours or more before boarding. Our team confirms the policy for your date before you pay.
