Overview

Dubai is a city where futuristic ambition and Eastern tradition exist side by side — glass towers rise beside sail-shaped icons born from Bedouin heritage.

This is a city that keeps reinventing itself: by day it dazzles with record-breaking skylines, by night it glows with fountains and lights reflected on the water.

What awaits you

  • Museum of the Future: a photo stop at the striking torus-shaped building that has become Dubai's newest symbol.
  • Burj Al Arab: a view of the legendary hotel rising against the sea.
  • Palm Jumeirah: a monorail ride across the man-made island with panoramic views of the coastline and skyscrapers.
  • Dubai Marina: cruise aboard a traditional dhow boat, with a meal served on board.
  • The Dubai Mall & singing fountains: the giant aquarium and an evening fountain show at the foot of Burj Khalifa.

Perfect for

  • First-time visitors: the city's main landmarks covered in a single day without planning your own route.
  • Families: a comfortable air-conditioned bus and an easy pace suited to guests of any age.
  • Photography lovers: the contrast between the futuristic skyline, the sail of Burj Al Arab and the marina lit up at sunset.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

Wear casual, closed clothing and comfortable shoes. Bring something warm — the bus is air-conditioned.

Is a return transfer included?

Yes, the return transfer is included in the price. If you decide to stay longer at The Dubai Mall or leave earlier, please let your guide know in advance.

What are the conditions for children?

Children under 3 years old travel free of charge, without a separate seat. Children aged 3 to 11 inclusive pay a child ticket; from age 12, the adult ticket applies. For the Burj Khalifa observation deck: children under 3 — free; ages 3 to 7 inclusive — child ticket; from age 8 — adult ticket.
