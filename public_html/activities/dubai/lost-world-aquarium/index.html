Overview

Explore the vibrant marine wonderland of the Lost World Aquarium in Dubai and immerse yourself in the beauty of the underwater world. Designed to transport you to a lost civilization beneath the waves, this aquarium is a must-visit attraction for marine enthusiasts and families alike. 

Observe the daily feeding sessions, where trained divers hand-feed the aquatic creatures, providing a unique spectacle. It offers interactive experiences that allow you to get up close and personal with some of the marine inhabitants. Visit the touch pools and have an amazing interactive experience with fascinating sea creatures. Gain valuable insights into marine conservation and the importance of preserving underwater ecosystems.  Book your Lost World Aquarium tickets now and embark on a journey to discover the wonders of the underwater world. It's an experience that will leave you with lasting memories.

Questions

About this experience

WHAT ARE THE OPENING HOURS OF THE LOST CHAMBERS AQUARIUM?

The aquarium is open daily from 10 AM to 10 PM. Last admission is typically an hour before closing.

CAN I PURCHASE TICKETS ON ARRIVAL OR SHOULD I BOOK IN ADVANCE?

While tickets can be purchased on arrival, we recommend booking in advance through our website to ensure availability and to take advantage of any online discounts or special offers.

ARE THERE ANY AGE RESTRICTIONS FOR VISITORS?

The Lost Chambers Aquarium is suitable for all ages. However, children under the age of 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Certain activities like diving may have age and health restrictions.

IS PHOTOGRAPHY ALLOWED INSIDE THE AQUARIUM?

Yes, photography for personal use is allowed and encouraged. However, flash photography is prohibited as it can disturb the marine life.

ARE THERE DINING FACILITIES WITHIN THE AQUARIUM?

While there are no dining facilities inside the aquarium, there are multiple dining options available within close proximity in Atlantis, The Palm.

IS THE LOST CHAMBERS AQUARIUM WHEELCHAIR ACCESSIBLE?

Yes, the aquarium is fully accessible for wheelchair users. Wheelchairs are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

IS THERE PARKING AVAILABLE AT THE LOST CHAMBERS AQUARIUM?

Yes, there is ample parking available at Atlantis, The Palm, which is where the Lost Chambers Aquarium is located.

CAN I CANCEL OR RESCHEDULE MY TICKETS?

Tickets are non-refundable.
