Overview

A shared yacht tour is an easy way to get out on the water in Dubai without hiring a whole boat. You join other guests on a luxury yacht of 70 feet and above, with a red-carpet welcome, a qualified crew and a lounge area with sun and shade.

The yacht has a dinette and three cabins with natural light. A BBQ is freshly prepared on board, with bottled water, Wi-Fi and the crew's help with photos. Choose a 2-hour dinner cruise in the evening, a 2-hour sunset cruise, or the 3-hour extended sunset cruise.

It is a sightseeing cruise, so there is no swimming, and alcohol is not permitted on board. For a boat just for your group, ask us about private yacht hire, or compare other yacht experiences.

Best for

  • Sunset photos: The sunset cruises are timed for the golden hour over the water.
  • Solo travellers and couples: A shared yacht costs far less than a private charter.
  • A relaxed evening: The dinner cruise pairs the city lights with a BBQ on deck.

About this experience

How much is a shared yacht tour in Dubai?

On KWIKAR, the shared yacht tour starts from AED 210 per person for the 2-hour dinner cruise. The sunset and 3-hour extended sunset cruises cost more; current prices are in the table above.

What is the difference between a shared and a private yacht?

On a shared yacht you book seats and sail with other guests, which costs much less. A private yacht is hired only for your group and can be planned around you. Ask us if you want a private yacht.

What times do the cruises run?

The dinner cruise runs 8:00–10:00pm or 9:00–11:00pm, the sunset cruise 5:30–7:30pm, and the extended sunset cruise 4:00–7:00pm. Our team confirms the slot for your date.

Is food included?

Yes. A freshly prepared BBQ and bottled water are included on every option.

Can I drink alcohol on board?

No. Alcohol is not permitted on this shared yacht tour.

Can I swim during the tour?

No. This is a sightseeing cruise, so swimming is not available.

What should I wear?

Smart casual. No bikinis, and shoes are not worn on board.

What do I need to bring?

Your passport or Emirates ID and your booking confirmation. Arrive 30 minutes before departure.

Is hotel pickup included?

No. Hotel transfers are not included, but we can arrange a private transfer to the marina.

Are life jackets available?

Yes. Life jackets are on board for anyone who wants to wear one.
