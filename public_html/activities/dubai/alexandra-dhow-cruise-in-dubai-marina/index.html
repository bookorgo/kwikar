Overview

A Dhow dinner cruise in Dubai Marina is an excellent opportunity to combine sightseeing and a dining experience. Alexandra dhow is the perfect floating restaurant, operating for more than 10 years with a proven track record.

Dubai Marina is in itself a world renowned land mark, and along our sailing route we will see the skyline of JBR, Blue Water Island, Dubai Harbour aswell as Cayan Tower and Ain Dubai.

Onboard you will have a full course dinner with the opportunity to order alcoholic drinks for an extra charge as well as a live entertainment program.

Questions

About this experience

Departure time?

Dinner Cruise Boarding at 20:30. Departure at 21:00. Sunset Cruise 17.00 boarding, 1730 departure.

Dress code?

Dress code is smart casual. In winter, it is desirable to have warm clothes.

Greetings for the birthday?

At the time of booking the cruise, the birthday boy/girl must be specified. Dessert will be provided at the table with congratulatory music during dinner. A cake can be ordered for an additional fee. To order, please contact our representative.

Child Policy?

Children under 5 years old are free (no seating on the bus on the group tour).  Children 5-12 years old - children's ticket.  From 12 years old - adult ticket.

Is it possible to reserve a certain table in advance?

Seats cannot be reserved in advance. There are free seats on arrival on first come first serve basis. We guarantee that every guest will have a seperate table on the deck which he has booked.
