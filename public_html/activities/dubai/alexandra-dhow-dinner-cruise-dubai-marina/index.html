Alexandra Dhow Dinner Cruise in Dubai Marina

Dubai Marina · 9:00–10:45pm · Buffet dinner and live entertainment

Meet at the boat or add hotel transferDinner and live entertainmentBook on WhatsApp

The Alexandra dhow is a traditional wooden boat that cruises Dubai Marina for 1 hour 45 minutes while you have a buffet dinner and watch live entertainment. Lower deck tables start from AED 180 per person; the upper deck and hotel transfers cost a little more. Boarding is from 8:15pm and the cruise sails 9:00–10:45pm.

Overview

A dhow is the traditional wooden boat of the Gulf, and an evening dinner cruise is one of the easiest ways to see the Dubai Marina skyline from the water. Alexandra has run its floating restaurant in the Marina for more than 10 years.

The cruise passes the towers of Dubai Marina and JBR, with views of Bluewaters Island, Ain Dubai, Dubai Harbour and Cayan Tower. On board you get a table on your chosen deck, an international buffet dinner with soft drinks and water, and live entertainment from a singer or dancer.

Choose the lower deck, which is enclosed, or the upper deck, which is open to the view. You can meet the boat at the Marina yourself or add a sharing or private hotel transfer. See all Dubai cruises to compare other boats.

Best for

  • A relaxed first evening: Dinner, a show and the skyline in under two hours.
  • Couples: Choose the upper deck for open-air views of the Marina at night.
  • Families: Children under 5 are free and the cruise is calm inside the Marina.

About this experience

How much is the Alexandra dhow cruise?

On KWIKAR, the Alexandra dinner cruise starts from AED 180 per person for a lower deck table when you meet at the boat. The upper deck and transfer options cost more; current prices for every option are in the table above.

What time does the dhow cruise start?

Boarding starts at 8:15pm, the boat departs at 9:00pm and returns at about 10:45pm. With a hotel transfer, pickup is between 7:00 and 8:15pm depending on your area.

Where do I board the Alexandra dhow?

In Dubai Marina, near Masjid Al Rahim and Signature Hotel Dubai Marina. Your confirmation includes the exact berth.

What is the difference between the upper and lower deck?

Both decks get the same dinner and show. The lower deck is enclosed; the upper deck is open, with clearer views of the Marina, and costs a little more.

What is the dress code?

Smart casual. In winter, bring a warm layer, especially for the upper deck.

What is the child policy?

Children under 5 are free, children aged 5 to 12 pay the child price, and guests 12 and over pay the adult price.

Can I reserve a specific table?

No. Seats are first come, first served, but every booking has its own table on the deck it booked.

Are alcoholic drinks available?

Yes, alcohol is sold on board at extra cost to guests aged 21 and over. Outside food and drinks are not allowed.

Can you arrange a birthday surprise?

Yes. Tell us the name when you book; the crew brings a dessert with music to your table. A cake can be ordered at extra cost.

Is hotel pickup included?

Only on the transfer options. Choose a sharing transfer, a private transfer for up to 6 guests within Dubai, or the sharing transfer from Sharjah and Ajman.
