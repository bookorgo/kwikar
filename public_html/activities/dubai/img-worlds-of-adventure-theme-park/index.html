Overview

IMG Worlds of Adventure – Dubai's Mega Indoor Theme Park

The world's largest indoor theme park, bringing Marvel superheroes, cartoon favourites, and life-sized dinosaurs under one giant roof.

Questions

About this experience

How do I purchase tickets for IMG Worlds of Adventure?

Tickets can be purchased online through our website or at the ticket counters at the park entrance. We recommend buying tickets online to ensure availability and to take advantage of any online-exclusive deals.

Are there any age or height restrictions for the rides?

Yes, some rides have age and height restrictions for safety reasons. These are clearly listed at the entrance to each ride. Please check the ride requirements before purchasing tickets.

What are the park's opening hours?

IMG Worlds of Adventure is open daily from 11 AM to 8 PM. However, these hours may vary during public holidays and special events. Please check our website for the most up-to-date information.

Is there a dress code for the park?

We recommend comfortable and appropriate attire suitable for a family-friendly environment. Please avoid clothing with offensive language or graphics. Closed shoes are advised for safety on rides.

Can I bring outside food and beverages?

Outside food and drinks are not permitted in the park. However, there are numerous dining options available inside to cater to a variety of tastes and dietary needs.

Are there facilities for visitors with disabilities?

Yes, IMG Worlds of Adventure is committed to providing a comfortable experience for all guests. We offer wheelchair rentals and have designed the park to be accessible for visitors with disabilities.

What should I do if I lose my belongings in the park?

Please contact the Lost & Found department located near the park entrance. 

Is parking available at the park?

Yes, we have ample parking available for guests.

Can I re-enter the park if I leave during the day?

Yes, guests can re-enter the park on the same day. Please keep your ticket or wristband on for re-entry.
