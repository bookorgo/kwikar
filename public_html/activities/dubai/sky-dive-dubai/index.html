Overview

Experience the thrill of a lifetime with our Sky Dive Dubai tour. Soar through the sky and feel the rush of adrenaline as you embark on a breathtaking skydiving adventure in one of the most iconic cities in the world.

As you ascend to the skies, take in the stunning panoramic views of Dubai's magnificent skyline, pristine beaches, and the vast Arabian Gulf. Feel the wind in your hair and the exhilaration in your heart as you freefall from thousands of feet above the ground.

Our experienced and certified instructors will guide you every step of the way, ensuring your safety and providing you with an unforgettable experience. Whether you're a seasoned skydiver or a first-timer, our team will make sure you have the time of your life.

After your thrilling skydive, take a moment to catch your breath and relish in the incredible accomplishment you've just achieved. Share your experience with friends and family as you receive a personalized certificate to commemorate your dive.

Questions

About this experience

Is skydiving safe?

Skydiving is a thrilling but safe activity when conducted with experienced instructors and proper safety measures in place. Our team of certified instructors will ensure your safety throughout the entire experience.

Can I bring my own camera to capture the skydiving experience?

For safety reasons, personal cameras or devices are not allowed during the skydiving experience. However, professional photographers will capture stunning photos and videos of your dive, which can be purchased afterward.

What happens if the weather is not suitable for skydiving?

The safety of our participants is our top priority. If weather conditions are deemed unsafe for skydiving, the tour may be rescheduled or canceled. Participants will be notified in advance and provided with alternative options.
