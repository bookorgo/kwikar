Overview

Experience the magic of flight with our Hot Air Balloon Flight in Dubai. This unforgettable adventure takes you soaring above the stunning Dubai landscape, offering breathtaking views of the desert and the majestic Hajar mountains. 

Our Hot Air Balloon Flight in Dubai is more than just a ride; it's an experience that will leave you feeling inspired and rejuvenated. As you float peacefully above the desert, you'll be captivated by the beauty of the sunrise, the vastness of the landscape, and the tranquility of the moment. 

We offer a variety of flight packages to suit all preferences. Our deluxe sunrise flights are a popular choice, allowing you to watch the sunrise over the mountains while soaring through the sky. If you're looking for a romantic getaway or a unique proposal idea, our private flights are perfect for you. 

Our experienced pilots are trained to provide a safe and enjoyable flight for all passengers. They will guide you through the skies, pointing out landmarks and sharing their knowledge of the area. We also happen to be the only company in the world offering an industry rare in-flight falcon show with all our packages. 

Our state-of-the-art hot air balloons are regularly maintained to ensure they meet the highest safety standards. They are equipped with the latest technology to ensure a comfortable and smooth ride. 

In addition to our standard flights, we also offer custom packages for corporate events, weddings, and other special occasions. Our team will work with you to create a unique experience that will be unforgettable for all. 

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply looking for a new experience, our Hot Air Balloon Flight in Dubai is the perfect adventure. Book now and secure a lifetime experience with us!

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

Casual, warm and comfortable clothing is strongly recommended.

Balloon flight altitude?

Flying altitude 1100-1200 meters. 

Restrictions on age and weight?

Must be 5 to 70 years old, and weighing no more than 120kg with no medical contraindications. 

What are the peculiarities of hot air balloon flight and landing?

If the weather is unfavorable, we will notify you as soon as possible.  The flight will be rescheduled or the excursion fee will be refunded.

Child policy?

Children 5-11 years old - child ticket.  Children 12 years and above - adult ticket. Children under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.
