Overview

LEGOLAND Water Park is the first water park in the region, created especially for families with children from 2 to 12 years old. Incredibly bright, colorful and unforgettable, thanks to its creative themes. The water park has over 20 water slides, including Joker Soaker and Splash Safari, where the atmosphere is that of a water holiday and a real outdoor activity.
Here you can plunge into the world of exciting journeys on the boat of your own design, compete with other inventors of unusual watercraft, feel proud of the waterways passed and dream about new adventures in this fantasy water space.

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

The uniform is beachwear. You must have swimwear and towels. There is an opportunity to buy swimwear in the water park stores, also on the territory operates towel rental.

Duration of the tour?

Time spent in the water park - 5 hours.

Can I bring water and food?

You can bring 0.5 liters of water to the park, food is prohibited. There are cafes in the park, where you can buy water and snacks.

What is the child policy?

Children under 3 years old free of charge (without providing a seat on the bus for the group tour).  From 3 years old - adult ticket. Documents confirming the age of the child may be required at the entrance. There are height restrictions for visiting some of the slides.  All young children must wear armbands and diapers to swim.
