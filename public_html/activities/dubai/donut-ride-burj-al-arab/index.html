Overview

​Experience Highlights

  • Skim across the waves and bounce through sharp turns on a giant inflatable donut.
  • Enjoy a fast-paced, high-energy activity in the sparkling waters of the Arabian Gulf.
  • Take in the breathtaking backdrop of Dubai’s iconic coastline during your ride.

​What to Expect:

The formula is pure fun: you and your crew will hop onto a giant, inflatable donut-shaped tube and hold on tight while a powerful jet ski does the rest. It is a laugh-out-loud adventure where you will try your absolute best to stay on as you glide over the waves.


​Perfect for:

  • Families.
  • Friend groups.
  • Anyone who loves a good splash and wants to fuel their Dubai vacation with laughter and adrenaline.

Questions

About this experience

What should I bring for water sport session?

You only need your ID or copy and swimwear.

Do I need any experience to ride?

No experience is required. Our instructors guide you step by step to ensure a safe and enjoyable ride.

How early should I arrive before my booking?

We recommend arriving 15–20 minutes earlier to complete registration and receive safety briefing.

Is photo and video included?

Your instructor can make photo and video for you during the ride.

Age policy

The minimum age for donut riding is 6 year sand above.

Safety precautions

All equipment is maintained to the highest marine standards, and every session is supervised by certified professionals.
