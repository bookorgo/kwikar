Overview

Aquaventure World at Atlantis The Palm in Dubai is one of the most iconic and expansive waterparks in the world, offering unforgettable experiences for both kids and adults. Spanning over 22 hectares, the park features an impressive variety of rides, attractions, and relaxing spaces — all themed around the lost city of Atlantis.

The waterpark is home to Trident Tower, Poseidon’s Tower, and Neptune’s Tower, each packed with record-breaking slides, wave pools, and rafting rides. Adrenaline seekers can try Leap of Faith, a near-vertical slide that sends you speeding through a clear tube surrounded by sharks. Families will love Splashers Lagoon and Cove, specially designed water playgrounds for younger children.

One of the park’s highlights is the Lost World Aquarium, a fascinating underwater world inspired by the ruins of Atlantis. Visitors can explore over 65,000 marine animals, including sharks, rays, and vibrant reef fish, in immersive tunnels and interactive exhibits.

The lush surroundings of the park are filled with palm trees, tropical flowers, and scenic lagoons. Guests also enjoy exclusive access to a private beach, where they can relax on the white sands with views of the Arabian Gulf.

Combining thrilling adventures with moments of serenity, Aquaventure is a must-visit for anyone seeking fun, discovery, and water-filled excitement in Dubai.

Questions

About this experience

What is the recommended dress code?

Beachwear is required. Please bring your swimwear and towels. Swimwear can be purchased at the waterpark's shops, and towel rental is also available on site.

What are the hours of operation?

The waterpark is open from 10.00 am to 18.30 pm. Please note the closing time of the park is not fixed and is based on the sunset timing, and we request you to double check the park timings before visiting the park.

Can I bring water and food?

You can bring up to 0.5 litres of water per person. Outside food is not permitted. There are cafes in the park where you can buy water and food.

What is the accepted age range?

Children under 1.2 m and under 3 years old are free. Children 3-7 years old - children's ticket. 8 years old and above - adult ticket. Documents confirming the age of the child may be required at the entrance. To visit some of the slides, must be latter than 1.2 m. For the little ones, there is a special play and swimming area called Splashers Island and Lagoon. All young children must wear armbands and swim diapers.

Can tickets be canceled or refunded?

Tickets cannot be canceled or refunded.

Is the park wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the park is accessible for wheelchair users.

Is there a play area for children?

Yes, there are dedicated play areas like Splashers Lagoon and Splashers Cove with shallow pools for children.

You can additionally purchase fast access to the rides/slides (AquaXpress).
