Overview

Aquaventure World is the waterpark at Atlantis The Palm, at the far end of Palm Jumeirah. It has 105 slides, attractions and experiences, from gentle rides on a 2.3 km tidal river to the Leap of Faith, a near-vertical drop from a 27.5-metre ziggurat that sends you through a tube in a lagoon full of sharks.

Younger children have their own water playground, Splashers, and the park also has a beach area, so a day can mix big slides with time to rest. Food and drink are sold inside the park at restaurants and kiosks; you cannot bring your own food or drinks in.

KWIKAR offers two tickets: an open-dated Aquaventure ticket for the park and its rides, and a dated Super Pass that adds The Lost World Aquarium at the same resort. Our team checks availability for your date before you pay. See also The Lost World Aquarium, more theme parks and things to do in Dubai.

Best for

  • Thrill seekers: Big slides, including the Leap of Faith through the shark lagoon.
  • Families: Splashers is a water playground for younger children, and life jackets are free for children under 1.2 m.
  • A flexible plan: The open-dated ticket lets you pick the day later.
  • Marine life fans: The Super Pass adds The Lost World Aquarium at Atlantis on the same date.
KWIKAR tip

Pack swimwear that follows the park's dress rules, and plan to buy lunch inside, as outside food is not allowed. If your children are under 1.2 m, check which slides they can use when you arrive.

Visitor information

Where to go
Aquaventure World is at Atlantis The Palm, at the end of Palm Jumeirah.
Opening hours
The park usually opens at 10am and closes at 6:30pm; closing times can change by season and around sunset. Our team confirms the times for your date.
Height rule
Children under 1.2 m must stay within arm's reach of a responsible adult in the water and wear a life jacket, which the park lends free of charge. Some slides have height limits.
Children
Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times. Child ticket rules depend on age and height; send the ages and heights of your children and our team confirms the right ticket.
Food and drink
Outside food, drinks, glassware and alcohol are not allowed. Snacks and meals are sold in the park.
What to wear
Proper swimwear is required in the water. Thongs, see-through swimsuits, underwear, denim and very loose clothing such as an abaya or kandoora are not allowed in the water; headscarves must be secured with no loose material.

About this experience

How much are Aquaventure tickets?

On KWIKAR, Aquaventure World tickets start from AED 360 per person. The Super Pass with The Lost World Aquarium costs more. Our team confirms the final price on WhatsApp before you pay.

Where is Aquaventure World?

At Atlantis The Palm, at the far end of Palm Jumeirah in Dubai.

What are the opening hours?

The park usually opens at 10am and closes at 6:30pm. Closing times can change by season, so our team confirms the hours for your date.

How many slides does Aquaventure have?

The park has 105 slides, attractions and experiences, including the Leap of Faith and a 2.3 km tidal river.

What is the Leap of Faith?

A near-vertical freefall slide that starts high on a 27.5-metre ziggurat and runs through a tube in a lagoon with sharks.

Is Aquaventure good for young children?

Yes. Splashers is a water playground for younger guests. Children under 1.2 m must stay within arm's reach of an adult in the water and wear a free life jacket, and some slides have height limits.

Is The Lost World Aquarium included?

Only with the Super Pass. The open-dated Aquaventure ticket covers the waterpark and its rides.

Can I bring food or drinks?

No. Outside food, drinks, glassware and alcohol are not allowed; there are restaurants and kiosks in the park.

What should I wear?

Proper swimwear. Underwear, denim, see-through swimsuits, thongs and very loose clothing are not allowed in the water.

Is hotel pickup included?

No, both options are entry tickets. We can add a private transfer from your hotel at extra cost; ask our team on WhatsApp.
