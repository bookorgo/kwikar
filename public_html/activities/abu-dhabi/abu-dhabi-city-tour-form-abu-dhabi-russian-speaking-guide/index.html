Overview

Abu Dhabi is a capital where soaring glass towers stand beside centuries-old traditions, and cutting-edge architecture exists in quiet dialogue with deep spiritual roots.

This tour reveals two faces of the city — one shaped by bold ambition and world-class art, the other by timeless faith and craftsmanship — closing with the sight of a dazzling white marble mosque.

What awaits you

  • Louvre Abu Dhabi: masterpieces of world art, unique collections and striking architecture under an iconic dome.
  • Date Market: taste and buy the finest varieties of this traditional delicacy.
  • Abu Dhabi Corniche: a photo stop with panoramic views of the capital's skyline.
  • Qasr Al Watan: the Presidential Palace, with luxurious interiors and grand reception halls.
  • Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: a majestic white marble mosque, a symbol of spirituality and architectural grandeur.

Perfect for

  • Guests staying in Abu Dhabi: a relaxed afternoon tour covering the capital's main highlights in one trip.
  • Culture and architecture lovers: a world-class museum, a presidential palace and a grand mosque in a single itinerary.
  • Photography lovers: the Corniche skyline, palace interiors and the white marble mosque offer striking contrasts for photos.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

Wear smart casual, modest clothing suitable for a museum, palace and mosque. Your clothing should be long — covering the ankles and wrists — non-transparent (not white) and without slits. If you visit the mosque, you should cover your head with a scarf, and any tattoos should be covered as well. Bring something warm, as the bus is air-conditioned.

Is a return transfer included?

Yes, a return transfer to your hotel is included in the price.

Is the abaya included in the price?

No, a traditional women's dress (abaya) for visiting the mosque can be purchased for an extra charge.

What are the conditions for children?

Children under 3 travel free of charge, without a separate seat on group tours. Children aged 3 to 11 inclusive pay a child ticket; from age 12, the adult ticket applies.
