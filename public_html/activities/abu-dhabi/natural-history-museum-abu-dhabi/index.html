Overview

Located in the iconic Saadiyat Cultural District, the Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi invites visitors on an extraordinary journey through time -  from the origins of the universe to the richness of life on Earth and the natural heritage of the Arabian Peninsula.

Designed by the award-winning architectural studio Mecanoo, the museum spans 35,000 sqm and features a striking form inspired by desert rock formations and the elemental patterns of life. Inside, breathtaking galleries bring together rare meteorites, exceptional dinosaur fossils -  including the world-famous Tyrannosaurus rex “Stan”  -  and immersive recreations of Abu Dhabi’s prehistoric landscapes.

More than a cultural landmark, the museum is a global centre for discovery and scientific advancement. Its state-of-the-art research facilities support pioneering work in palaeontology, biodiversity, marine science, and earth sciences.

Through interactive exhibitions, family programmes, workshops, and community-science initiatives, the Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi inspires curiosity, sparks imagination, and encourages visitors of all ages to engage with the natural world and envision a more sustainable future.

Discover the story of life - shaped by the past, designed for the future.

What to expect

  • Galleries: rare meteorites and exceptional dinosaur fossils, including the world-famous Tyrannosaurus rex “Stan”.
  • Architecture: a striking 35,000 sqm building by Mecanoo, inspired by desert rock formations and the elemental patterns of life.
  • Prehistoric landscapes: immersive recreations of Abu Dhabi’s ancient natural heritage.
  • Science hub: state-of-the-art research facilities supporting work in paleontology, biodiversity, marine science, and earth sciences.
  • Interactive programmes: family activities, workshops and community-science initiatives for all ages.

Perfect for

  • Families with children: interactive galleries and hands-on activities suitable for all ages.
  • Science and nature enthusiasts: rare fossils, meteorites, and cutting-edge research displays.
  • Culture and architecture lovers: a striking building by Mecanoo in the heart of the Saadiyat Cultural District.
  • Curious minds of all ages: immersive storytelling about the origins of life and the universe.

About this experience

Dress code

Dress modestly, ensuring that shoulders and knees are covered. Comfortable footwear is recommended, as the visit involves considerable walking. It is also recommended to bring a light jacket or outerwear, as the museum is air-conditioned.

Experience duration

The average visit duration is approximately 2 to 3 hours, depending on the number of visitors.

Child Policy

Children under 18 years old – free of charge. Visitors aged 18 and above – adult rate applies. Children under the age of 13 must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or older. Proof of the child's age may be required.

Where is the Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi located?

Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi is situated on Saadiyat Island, within the Saadiyat Cultural District in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

Do I need to print the ticket?

Both printed and electronic confirmations are accepted.

Required Documents

A valid ticket (either printed or electronic) is required for entry.

Are guests permitted to bring outside food and beverages?

Visitors are not allowed to bring food and beverages.
