Overview

Seen from above, Abu Dhabi is a capital where glass skyscrapers sit alongside the turquoise waters of the Persian Gulf and the untouched mangroves of its islands.

The city reveals itself in a whole new way: the Corniche and grand palace complexes give way to the natural landscapes of Al Hudayriat and Reem islands, while the silhouette of the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque looks even more majestic from the air.

What to Expect

  • The Corniche and capital landmarks: a bird's-eye view of the waterfront, Marina Mall, Emirates Palace hotel and the Presidential Palace.
  • Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: the majestic silhouette of Abu Dhabi's most famous landmark, which can only be fully appreciated from above.
  • Islands and mangroves: Al Hudayriat and Reem islands set against the sparkling waters of the Persian Gulf.
  • Record-breaking architecture: the leaning Capital Gate tower and a 123-metre flagpole.
  • Ideal For
  • Special occasions: mark a memorable date with an unforgettable flight over the capital — special birthday and marriage proposal packages are available.
  • First-time visitors and family trips: see Abu Dhabi's top attractions from a completely new angle in just a few minutes.
  • Photography lovers and thrill-seekers: stunning shots and the adrenaline rush of flying high, with professional photography available.

Questions

About this experience

What should I wear?

The dress code is casual. Hats and scarves are not allowed during the flight.

What can I take on board?

Only a phone, camera and sunglasses are allowed. Bags, hats and scarves must be left in the storage area at the helipad. Professional cameras with lenses of 400 mm or longer, selfie sticks and tablets are not permitted on board.

Are there any weight restrictions?

Yes. On shared flights, the weight limit is 110 kg per passenger; passengers over this limit must purchase an additional seat. On private flights, the total weight limit is 450 kg. Seatbelt extenders are not used.

Can pregnant women take the flight?

Yes, up to the 32nd week of pregnancy. We recommend getting your doctor's approval beforehand.

What happens in bad weather?

Flights may operate in reduced visibility. If a flight is cancelled due to weather conditions, you will be offered a new date or a refund.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No, both printed and electronic tickets are accepted.

Where is the helipad located?

Flights depart from the helipad at Zayed Port in Abu Dhabi.

What are the rules for children?

Children under 2 are not permitted to fly. Children aged 2 and over require their own seat and pay the full adult fare. Children aged 2–12 must be accompanied by an adult (18 or over).

Can I drive the helicopter myself?

It is not allowed to fly a helicopter by law.
