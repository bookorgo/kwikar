Overview

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi is an indoor theme park on Yas Island, built around the Ferrari brand. Under its red roof you will find roller coasters, racing-themed rides and simulators, family rides and displays of Ferrari cars, so most of the day is spent out of the sun and in air conditioning.

The park is best known for Formula Rossa, a launched coaster that reaches 240 km/h and goes from 0 to 100 km/h in about two seconds. The park's ride updates page currently lists Formula Rossa as closed for maintenance, with no reopening date, so please do not plan your visit around it. Other rides can also close without notice; the park asks guests to check the ride schedule on arrival.

KWIKAR sells the open-dated general admission ticket, which gives all-day access to the rides, and a Fast Track add-on for shorter queues. Our team checks your request and confirms the final price before you pay. Yas Island has more parks next door: Warner Bros. World, Yas Waterworld and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi. See also things to do in Abu Dhabi.

Best for

  • Car and racing fans: Ferrari-themed rides, simulators and car displays.
  • Hot days: The park is indoors, so it works well in the summer months.
  • A flexible plan: The open-dated ticket lets you pick the day later.
  • A Yas Island day: Combine it with Warner Bros. World, Yas Waterworld or SeaWorld nearby.
KWIKAR tip

Formula Rossa is closed for maintenance for now, so check the park's ride updates before you go. If you want to visit several Yas Island parks, ask about the multi-park ticket.

About this experience

How much are Ferrari World tickets?

The live price of each option is shown in the ticket list on this page. The Fast Track add-on is bought on top of a general admission ticket. Our team confirms the final price on WhatsApp before you pay.

Is Formula Rossa open?

Not at the moment. The park's ride updates page lists Formula Rossa as closed for maintenance, with no reopening date given. Our team can check the latest status for your date.

Where is Ferrari World?

On Yas Island in Abu Dhabi, near Warner Bros. World, Yas Waterworld and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi.

Is Ferrari World indoors?

Yes, it is an indoor theme park, which makes it a good choice on hot days.

How long is the ticket valid?

The general admission ticket is open-dated and valid for at least one month, so you can choose the day later.

Do children need a ticket?

Children under 2 enter free. Send the ages of your children and our team confirms the tickets you need.

Are there height limits?

Yes, some rides have height limits for safety. The supplier lists limits of above 1.4 m and below 1.95 m for some attractions.

What does Fast Track include?

Fast track access to the rides. It is an add-on and does not include park entry, so you also need a general admission ticket.

Is food included?

No. Meals and drinks are not included and are sold inside the park.

Is hotel pickup included?

No, these are entry tickets. We can add a private transfer from Dubai or Abu Dhabi at extra cost; ask our team on WhatsApp.
