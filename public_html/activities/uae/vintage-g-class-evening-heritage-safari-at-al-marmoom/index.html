Overview

As the afternoon heat fades, the Al Marmoom Desert Conservation Reserve turns gold — the largest unfenced reserve in the UAE, where ghaf trees, oryx and gazelles share the dunes with a quiet oasis.

You cross it the classic way: in an open-top vintage Mercedes G-Class, the wind in your face and the sand stretching to the horizon, before the evening slows down to the pace of Bedouin life.

What to expect

  • Vintage G-Class drive: a 30-40 minute ride through the reserve in an open-top classic 4x4, with a guide who points out desert plants and wildlife.
  • Camel caravan: a short ride across the dunes at sunset, guided by Bedouin handlers.
  • Falconry show: watch a live falcon display and take a photo with the bird on your arm.
  • Bedouin welcome: Arabic coffee, dates and sweets in a traditional village, with henna painting and live cooking.
  • Five-course dinner under the stars: a set Bedouin menu from the Michelin Bib Gourmand Al Khayma Heritage Restaurant.
  • Stargazing: finish the night at the telescopes and learn how Bedouins used the stars to cross the desert.

Perfect for

  • Couples: a calm, romantic desert evening without the noise of a dune-bashing camp.
  • Families: gentle activities and a real look at Emirati heritage.
  • Photographers: vintage cars, camels, falcons and sunset light in one evening.

Questions

About this experience

What should I wear?

Comfortable, casual clothes and closed shoes such as trainers. Bring a warm layer from November to March, and sunglasses.

Can children join?

Yes. Children 4-11 pay the child rate and children under 4 join free. Children under 12 do not ride the camels alone; an adult stays with them.

Who should not join?

Pregnant women, guests with back or neck problems and wheelchair users.

Is the return transfer included?

Yes, the drop-off at your hotel in Dubai is part of the tour.

Can I drive the G-Class?

No, a licensed driver-guide drives the vehicle.
