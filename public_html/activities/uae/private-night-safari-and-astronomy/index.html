Private Night Safari and Astronomy

UAE · Desert Tours & Activities

Overview

See the desert when it comes to life after dark. Your private car and Conservation Guide take you into the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve for a 60-minute night drive in a 1950s Land Rover, followed by a guided eco-walk with torches and UV lights to find sandfish, scorpions and other night creatures.

At a peaceful, torch-lit oasis on royal desert grounds you join other guests for Arabic coffee, a close look at desert owls and a three-course dinner. The evening ends with an astronomy session at a telescope and stories of how the Bedouin used the stars to find their way. You can add a night in a stone dwelling and a sunrise balloon flight.

What to expect

  • Private night drive: 60 minutes in a vintage Land Rover with your own guide.
  • Nocturnal eco-walk: search for desert wildlife with torches and UV lights.
  • Owl encounter: learn about desert owls and see them up close.
  • Three-course dinner: Emirati and grill dishes cooked fresh on site.
  • Astronomy session: the moon, planets and constellations through a telescope, with an expert.
  • Options: a night in a stone dwelling with breakfast, and a sunrise hot air balloon flight (November to April).

Perfect for

  • Couples: a private, romantic evening under the stars.
  • Families: children love the night walk, the owls and the telescope.
  • Curious travellers: a different side of the desert, far from the busy safari camps.

How it works

  1. Private pick-up in Dubai

    Pick-up between 17:00 and 18:30, depending on the season.

  2. Night drive in a vintage Land Rover

    A private 60-minute nature drive after sunset.

  3. Nocturnal eco-walk

    Look for desert creatures with torches and UV lights.

  4. Dinner at the oasis

    Arabic coffee, an owl encounter and a three-course dinner.

  5. Astronomy session

    Stars, planets and Bedouin star stories at the telescope.

  6. Return or stay overnight

    Drop-off 22:00-23:30, or a night in a stone dwelling (and a sunrise balloon flight) with the overnight options.

Questions

About this experience

Is the tour private?

The car, the guide and the night drive are private. Dinner and the astronomy session are at a shared camp.

What if it is cloudy?

You still get an astronomy talk; on clear nights you use the telescope.

Can we stay overnight?

Yes, from 1 November to 30 April, with breakfast. You can add a sunrise balloon flight (minimum age 7).

What should I wear?

Light, comfortable clothes, a hat, sunglasses and sunscreen. From November to March bring a warm layer for the evening and the early morning.

Can you cater for special diets?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and kosher-style meals are possible. Tell us about diets and allergies when you book.
