New Year's Eve Dinner at Sky Views Observatory, Dubai

Downtown Dubai · Thursday 31 December 2026 · 8pm to midnight · Burj Khalifa fireworks

Burj Khalifa fireworks31 December onlyBook on WhatsApp

New Year's Eve at Sky Views Observatory is a seated dinner on floors 52 and 53 of Address Sky View, right next to the Burj Khalifa, on Thursday 31 December 2026 from 8pm to midnight. Starters are shared on the table, then you pick a main course and a dessert, and drinks from your package are served from 8pm to 11pm. You choose one of four packages: non-alcoholic, house wine and beer, premium drinks, or all drinks plus the Edge Walk on the open-air ledge during the fireworks. Children under 3 are free. Send your request on WhatsApp and our team confirms availability and the final price before you pay.

Overview

Sky Views Observatory sits on the 52nd and 53rd floors of Address Sky View in Downtown Dubai, right beside the Burj Khalifa. On New Year's Eve the observatory becomes a dinner venue: guests sit at shared tables by the floor-to-ceiling windows, with the glass-floor walkway and observation deck around them and live entertainment through the evening.

Dinner starts with cold and hot starters served to the table to share, such as mezze, sushi rolls, shrimp ceviche and chicken bites. Each guest then chooses one main course (salmon, chicken, beef medallion or a vegetarian pasta) and one dessert. Drinks from your package are served from 8pm to 11pm; after that, any drinks are charged separately. The menu can change with the season, so tell us about allergies when you send your request.

At midnight the Burj Khalifa fireworks go off almost in front of you. Guests on the Edge Walk package step outside onto the open-air ledge to watch the countdown from the edge of the tower. For a daytime visit on another date, see our Sky Views Dubai tickets, or compare other ways to see in the New Year at the Alexandra New Year's Eve gala dinner on the water.

Best for

  • Couples: A seated dinner high above Downtown with the fireworks at midnight.
  • Friends: Drinks packages with wine and beer, or premium drinks with sparkling wine.
  • Thrill seekers: The Edge Walk package puts you on the outside ledge during the countdown.
KWIKAR tip

Send your request early: seats are limited. Arrive well before 8pm, because roads around Downtown Dubai start closing in the afternoon on New Year's Eve.

Visitor information

Entrance
Enter through Address Sky View Tower 1, next to the residents' entrance, or walk in from The Dubai Mall side via the Dubai Metro link. You need the guest's first and last name when you book; our team sends the event-day badge and entry details before 31 December.
Getting there on New Year's Eve
Downtown Dubai is the busiest place in the city that night. In past years the RTA closed roads around the Burj Khalifa in phases from 4pm, closed Sheikh Zayed Road near Downtown later in the evening and ran the Metro through the night. Plan to arrive early, use the Metro or a private car to Downtown Dubai, and check the RTA plan for 2026 closer to the date.
Edge Walk rules
The Edge Walk is open to guests aged 12 to 65 who weigh 30 to 120 kg and meet the height limit; under-18s need a parent or guardian with them to give consent. Tell the staff about any heart, back or neck condition, epilepsy, motion sickness or pregnancy. Loose dresses, skirts, flip-flops and long scarves are not allowed on the ledge, and outdoor experiences can close in bad weather.
Alcohol and age
Alcohol is served only to guests aged 21 and over, and ID can be requested. Children under 3 are free. Send the ages in your group and our team confirms the best package for older children.

About this experience

When is the Sky Views New Year's Eve dinner?

On Thursday 31 December 2026, from 8pm to midnight. The offer is only valid on 31 December.

Can you see the Burj Khalifa fireworks from Sky Views?

Yes. Sky Views Observatory is on floors 52–53 of Address Sky View, right next to the Burj Khalifa, so the midnight fireworks are in front of you. Edge Walk guests watch them from the open-air ledge.

How much does New Year's Eve at Sky Views cost?

The price depends on the package: the non-alcoholic package costs the least and the Edge Walk package the most. Our team confirms the final price before you pay.

What is included in the dinner?

Unlimited cold and hot starters shared on the table, one main course and one dessert per guest, drinks from your package from 8pm to 11pm, a New Year's Eve souvenir and the fireworks view.

Until what time are drinks served?

Drinks in your package are served from 8pm to 11pm. Any other drinks, or drinks after 11pm, are charged separately.

Can children come to the Sky Views New Year's Eve dinner?

Children under 3 are free. Alcohol is only served to guests aged 21 and over, and the Edge Walk is only for ages 12 to 65. Send the ages in your group and our team confirms what suits you.

Who can do the Edge Walk on New Year's Eve?

Guests on the Edge Walk package aged 12 to 65 who weigh 30 to 120 kg and meet the height limit. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to give consent, and the Edge Walk can close in bad weather.

Is the seating private?

No. Seating is at shared tables. Ask our team if you would like to be seated together as a group.

How do I get to Sky Views on New Year's Eve?

Enter through Address Sky View Tower 1 or from The Dubai Mall side. Roads around Downtown close in phases on New Year's Eve, so arrive early and use the Metro or a private car that drops you before the closures.
