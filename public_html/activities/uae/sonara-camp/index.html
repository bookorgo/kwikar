Overview

Discover the enchanting Sonara Camp, nestled in the heart of the breathtaking Arabian Desert within the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve. Combining luxury with sustainability, Sonara offers an extraordinary dining experience set against an awe-inspiring natural backdrop. Driven by eco-friendly principles, the camp utilizes solar energy, practices zero-waste policies, and sources materials and ingredients ethically, ensuring your visit supports responsible tourism.

Experience the magic of Sonara Camp at sunset. A short drive across the dunes will take you to a cozy lounge with panoramic desert views, where refreshing drinks and light bites are served to the sounds of ambient music. Guests can enjoy a variety of activities: sandboarding, volleyball, soft archery, a camel ride, and a captivating falcon show – a true symbol of Arabian culture.

In the evening, Sonara Camp transforms into an enchanting dining destination among the dunes. Your journey begins with a scenic desert drive, setting the tone for a magical night. At the camp, indulge in a refined seasonal menu crafted by the chef, inspired by the rich flavors of local cuisine. Entertainment awaits guests of all ages: sandboarding, volleyball, and archery, while the evening comes alive with a falconry show, fire performance, music, and for children – a movie under the stars.

Choose your perfect Sonara experience: Sunset, Dinner, or the ultimate combination of both.

Sonara Camp promises more than just a dinner – it’s an extraordinary adventure connecting you deeply with nature, culture, and unforgettable desert hospitality.
