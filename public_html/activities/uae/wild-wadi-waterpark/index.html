Overview

Unforgettable aquatic adventure at Dubais original water park!

Inspired by the legendary voyages of Sinbad the Sailor, the park spans over 5 hectares and features more than 25 water slides and attractions, including the mesmerizing 18-meter Wadi Wash waterfall that dazzles with light and music.

Wild Wadi has one of the world's tallest water slides at a staggering 32 meters high! Brace yourself for the ultimate rush as you descend in specially designed capsules at speeds of up to 80 km/h and experience the thrill of free fall. For those who prefer to catch a wave, our 1.5-meter high waves are perfect for surfing.

With so much excitement packed into one place, Wild Wadi water park promises to be a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Don't miss out on the opportunity to create unforgettable memories with family and friends.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

The dress code is beachwear You is recommended to bring your own swimwear and towels, however it is possible to purchase swimwear at the water park stores, and towel rental is available on-site.

Can I bring my own food and drinks into the park?

Outside food and drinks are not permitted inside the park, with the exception of baby food and essential dietary items. There is a variety of dining options available inside the park to cater to your needs.

Child policy

Children under 2 years old are free of charge Children below 1.1 meters in height need a child ticket Above 1.1 meters - adult ticket ID confirming the child's age may be required at the entrance Some slides have height restrictions for visitors For Toddlers, there is a specially designated area for play and swimming called Juha's Dhow and Lagoon Toddlers must wear swimming arm bands and diapers

Can I refund my ticket?

Tickets are not refundable.

What are the opening hours of Wild Wadi Waterpark?

The park's opening hours vary by season. Typically, we open at 10:00 AM and close at 6:00 PM. For the most accurate opening hours, please check our website or contact customer service.

Are there any height or age restrictions for the rides?

Yes, some rides have height and age restrictions for safety reasons. These restrictions are listed on our website and at the entrance of each ride.

What amenities are available inside the park?

Wild Wadi Waterpark offers locker rentals, towel rentals, multiple dining options, and gift shops. Additionally, there are relaxation areas and cabanas available for rent.

Is parking available at the park?

Yes, we offer free parking for guests. Parking spaces are available on a first-come, first-served basis.
