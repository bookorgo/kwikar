New Year's Eve in Dubai 2026: Where to Watch the Fireworks

By the KWIKAR team · Published 14 Nov 2026 · Updated 14 Nov 2026

The biggest New Year's Eve show in Dubai is the Burj Khalifa fireworks at midnight on Thursday 31 December 2026. You can watch for free from the public viewing areas in Downtown Dubai if you arrive before the roads close in the afternoon, or from beaches and other venues with their own fireworks: last year Dubai had 48 shows at 40 locations. For a seat and dinner, choose a New Year's Eve dinner at Sky Views next to the Burj Khalifa or a gala cruise that is on the water at midnight.

Thursday 31 December 2026, countdown at midnight

The two New Year's Eve events are priced by package or deck for 31 December only: our team confirms the price for your choice when you send your request. The regular Marina dhow cruise ends at 10:45pm, so it suits people who want dinner on the water first and then watch the midnight fireworks from a public spot.

Sky Views Observatory: dinner right next to the Burj Khalifa

Sky Views sits on the 52nd and 53rd floors of Address Sky View in Downtown Dubai, beside the Burj Khalifa. On New Year's Eve it becomes a dinner venue from 8pm to midnight: shared starters, then a main course and dessert each, with drinks from your package served until 11pm. There are four packages, from non-alcoholic to the Edge Walk package, where you watch the countdown from the open-air ledge (ages 12 to 65 only). Children under 3 are free and alcohol is only served to guests aged 21 and over. You have a table indoors instead of a standing spot in the crowds. Full details are on our New Year's Eve at Sky Views page.

A New Year's Eve cruise: fireworks from the water

The Alexandra New Year's Eve gala dinner is a three-hour cruise that leaves at 10pm, so you are on the water at midnight. You choose the boat (the Alexandra 2 dhow or the Alexandra Sea Lounge), the upper deck or the lower deck, and whether to add a shared hotel transfer, which saves you looking for a taxi on the busiest night of the year. Dinner is a buffet with a special New Year's Eve menu and a tanura dance and magic show.

Which fireworks you see depends on where the boat is at midnight. The operator's plan describes a cruise into the Business Bay canal for the Burj Khalifa display, but routes on the night can change, so ask our team which viewing spot applies before you book if the Burj Khalifa view matters most. Our dhow cruise vs yacht guide explains the boats for other evenings.

Free places to watch the fireworks

Downtown Dubai public viewing areas: Emaar runs free public viewing areas around the Burj Khalifa on a first-come, first-served basis, with no ticket or badge needed. Last year there were seven gates: Boulevard Heights, Burj Vista, Emaar Square, the former Address Boulevard, Fountain Views, Address Downtown and Vida Dubai Mall. Access is controlled and each gate closes when its area is full, so arrive in the afternoon. Food and non-alcoholic drinks are allowed, and large LED screens line Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard. Emaar also sells tickets for a paid area in Burj Park.

Other fireworks across Dubai: the Burj Khalifa is not the only show. For the start of 2026, Dubai had 48 fireworks shows at 40 locations, and the official list included:

  • Beaches and the Palm: Atlantis The Palm, Burj Al Arab, The Beach at JBR and J1 Beach at La Mer.
  • Old Dubai: Al Seef on Dubai Creek and the Dubai Frame, which also had a drone show.
  • Family venues and further out: Global Village (with several countdowns timed to other countries' midnights), Expo City Dubai, Dubai Parks and Resorts, Bab Al Shams desert resort, Al Marmoom Oasis and Hatta.

Watching from a beach or venue near your hotel avoids the Downtown road closures. The list for 31 December 2026 is not out yet: last year it was published on 26 December, and we will update this guide when it is.

Getting there and back on New Year's Eve

  • Road closures: last year the RTA closed roads around Downtown Dubai in phases from 4pm, extending until 11pm, when part of Sheikh Zayed Road closed. Once the roads are closed, taxis and cars cannot get into Downtown.
  • Dubai Metro: the Metro ran non-stop for 43 hours, from 5am on 31 December until the end of 1 January, with extra trains at stations such as Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall, Business Bay and Financial Centre. Stations are very busy after midnight.
  • Private car: a chauffeur by the hour can drop you before the closures; our team plans the pickup point for the way back with you. Arriving from the airport that day? Send a request for a DXB to Downtown Dubai transfer that gets you there well before 4pm.
  • Cruise transfers: on the Alexandra gala, the sharing transfer options pick you up between about 7pm and 9pm and bring you back after the cruise.

The RTA and Dubai Police publish the plan for 2026 closer to the date, and our team shares the latest times with your booking.

Which option suits you?

  • The Burj Khalifa fireworks up close, with a seat and dinner: Sky Views Observatory.
  • Dinner, a show and midnight on the water: the Alexandra gala cruise, with a sharing transfer if you do not want to drive.
  • On a budget or with small children: an early Marina dhow dinner, then a beach such as JBR, or a free Downtown viewing area if you can arrive early.
  • A full family day: Global Village had its own fireworks; see our Global Village guide for timings.

Late December is a busy time in Dubai, so send your request early. Our guide to the best time to visit Dubai covers the weather and other events, and you can see the city from above on another evening with Burj Khalifa At the Top tickets.

Questions

Where is the best place to watch the New Year fireworks in Dubai?

For the Burj Khalifa show, the closest places are the Downtown Dubai public viewing areas (free, first come, first served), Burj Park (paid) and venues next to the tower such as Sky Views Observatory. For a calmer night, watch one of the other displays from a beach such as JBR, or from a cruise on the water.

What time are the New Year fireworks in Dubai?

The main fireworks start at midnight, as 31 December 2026 turns into 1 January 2027. Arrive in Downtown Dubai in the afternoon: in past years the roads around the Burj Khalifa started to close at about 4pm.

Can I watch the Burj Khalifa fireworks for free?

Yes. Emaar's public viewing areas in Downtown Dubai are free and need no ticket or badge, but entry is first come, first served and each gate closes when its area is full. Food and non-alcoholic drinks are allowed.

Where else are there fireworks in Dubai on New Year's Eve?

For the start of 2026 there were 48 shows at 40 locations, including Atlantis The Palm, Burj Al Arab, The Beach at JBR, La Mer, Al Seef, the Dubai Frame, Global Village, Expo City Dubai and Hatta. The list is usually published a few days before New Year's Eve.

Does the Dubai Metro run all night on New Year's Eve?

Last year the Dubai Metro ran non-stop for 43 hours, from 5am on 31 December until the end of 1 January. The RTA announces the 2026 timings closer to the date.

Can you see the Burj Khalifa fireworks from a dinner cruise?

It depends on where the boat is at midnight. The Alexandra New Year's Eve gala plans a cruise into the Business Bay canal for the Burj Khalifa display, but routes can change on the night, so ask our team before you book if this view is a must.

How much is New Year's Eve dinner at Sky Views or on a gala cruise?

Both events are priced by package: at Sky Views the non-alcoholic package costs the least and the Edge Walk package the most; on the Alexandra gala, the lower deck without a transfer costs the least. Send your request on WhatsApp and our team confirms the final price before you pay.

Is New Year's Eve in Dubai good for families?

Yes, but Downtown is very crowded. Families often choose a beach, Global Village or another venue with its own fireworks, or a seated option such as Sky Views (children under 3 free) or a cruise (send the children's ages and our team confirms the rules).
