Ferrari World vs Warner Bros. World vs Yas Waterworld

By the KWIKAR team · Published 11 Nov 2026 · Updated 11 Nov 2026

Choose Ferrari World for thrill rides and racing, Warner Bros. World for a family day with cartoon and superhero lands, and Yas Waterworld for slides and pools in the cooler months. The first two are indoor and air-conditioned; a multi-park ticket lets you visit more than one.

Ferrari World: for speed and thrills

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi sits under a large red roof linked to Yas Mall. It is fully indoors and air-conditioned, and the park is open every day of the year. The rides are built around racing: launched coasters, driving simulators, family rides and displays of Ferrari cars.

  • Ride closures: the park currently lists its best-known coaster, Formula Rossa, as closed for maintenance, and the Roof Walk is unavailable until further notice. Check the ride list on the day rather than planning around one ride.
  • Who it suits: older children, teenagers and adults who like fast rides. Younger children have their own rides, but the park shines for thrill seekers.
  • Getting there by car: parking is free in the Grand Prix car park at Yas Mall.

Warner Bros. World: for families and fans

Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi is a large indoor theme park, also fully air-conditioned and open every day. It is split into themed lands, including Metropolis, Gotham City, Cartoon Junction, Bedrock and Dynamite Gulch, with rides, shows and characters from Warner Bros. films and cartoons.

  • Who it suits: mixed-age families. Small children enjoy the cartoon lands, while older children and adults head for the superhero rides in Gotham City and Metropolis.
  • What to wear: the park asks for respectful clothing and recommends shorts that reach the knee and covered shoulders.
  • Pace: with shows and character meetings as well as rides, plan most of the day here.

Yas Waterworld: for a day in the water

Yas Waterworld is a water park with slides, family rides, a surf pool and a river ride. Unlike the other two parks, much of your day is spent outside in the sun, so it is most comfortable in the cooler months, roughly October to April.

  • Swimwear: suitable swimwear must be worn at all times. Denim, underwear and clothes with exposed metal are not allowed on the water attractions. Lockers and towels can be rented at the park.
  • Food: outside food and drinks are not allowed in the park; there are cafés inside.
  • Safety: life jackets are free to use, but inflatable armbands and rings are not allowed.
  • Maintenance: water parks close some rides for maintenance in the quieter months. In November 2026 the park listed several rides as closed, including Falcon's Falaj and Dawwama, so ask our team which rides are open for your date.

Children and height rules

All three parks give free entry to children under 3 when they come with their parents. Ticket prices at the parks are based on height: guests under 1.1 m are on the lower price, and some rides have their own height limits, checked by staff at each ride. Send the ages and heights of your children and our team confirms the right tickets for your group.

Visiting more than one park

The parks are close together on Yas Island, so you can combine them. The Yas Island multi-park ticket comes in several versions: two parks in one day, or passes for two, three or four parks, where you have six days after your first park to visit the others. SeaWorld Abu Dhabi is also included in the multi-park choice. Two parks in one day works best with one indoor park and Yas Waterworld, or two indoor parks, as each park takes several hours.

Each park also sells a Fast Track add-on for shorter queues. It is bought on top of a general admission ticket and does not include entry on its own.

Getting to Yas Island from Dubai

Yas Island is in Abu Dhabi, so plan a full day out from Dubai. The parks run free Yas Express shuttle buses from Dubai and some other emirates for ticket holders; routes and times change, so check them before you go. Which one should you choose?

  • Choose Ferrari World if you want the fastest rides and love cars, or you are visiting in the hot months.
  • Choose Warner Bros. World for a family day with characters, shows and rides for all ages, also indoors.
  • Choose Yas Waterworld for slides and pools in the cooler months, when a day outside is pleasant.
  • Choose the multi-park ticket if your group cannot decide or you have more than one day on Yas Island.

Questions

Which is better, Ferrari World or Warner Bros. World?

Ferrari World suits thrill seekers and car fans. Warner Bros. World suits families with children of different ages, with cartoon lands for little ones and superhero rides for older children. Both are indoor and air-conditioned.

Is Yas Waterworld indoors?

No. It is a water park and much of the day is spent outside, so it is most comfortable from about October to April. Ferrari World and Warner Bros. World are the indoor choices for hot days.

Can I visit two Yas Island parks in one day?

Yes, with the multi-park ticket for two parks in one day. Each park takes several hours, so start early. Passes for two, three or four parks over several days are also available.

Do children go free at the Yas Island parks?

Children under 3 enter free with their parents at all three parks. Guests under 1.1 m pay the lower ticket price. Send ages and heights and our team confirms the tickets.

Is Formula Rossa open?

Ferrari World currently lists Formula Rossa as closed for maintenance, with no reopening date. Our team can check the latest status for your date.

How do I get to Yas Island from Dubai?

The parks run free Yas Express shuttle buses from Dubai for ticket holders, or you can book a private transfer from Dubai to Abu Dhabi with our team.

Can I bring food into Yas Waterworld?

No. Outside food and drinks are not allowed; there are cafés inside the park.
