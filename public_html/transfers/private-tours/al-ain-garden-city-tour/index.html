Private Al Ain Garden City Tour

Full day · 10 hours · Al Ain (Abu Dhabi)

Price per vehicleTolls and parking includedBook on WhatsApp

Al Ain is the green inland city of Abu Dhabi, the birthplace of Sheikh Zayed and a UNESCO World Heritage site. This private day trip from Dubai combines palm oases, forts and the drive up Jebel Hafeet. Full day tour of up to 10 hours, from AED 1,150 per car (Business Sedan). Included: your own car and chauffeur for the whole tour, hotel pickup and drop-off in Dubai, fuel, Salik and Darb tolls, parking and child seats. Not included: entrance tickets, food and drinks, optional activities and a licensed tour guide (your chauffeur drives; he is not a guide).

Suggested stops

A private tour: change the order, skip stops or stay longer where you like.

01

Al Ain Oasis

Shady paths through thousands of date palms and old falaj water channels.

02

Al Jahili Fort

One of the largest forts in the UAE, with a small exhibition.

03

Al Ain Palace Museum

Sheikh Zayed's former home, now a museum.

04

Camel market

One of the last traditional camel markets in the country (mornings).

05

Jebel Hafeet

Drive the mountain road to the top for the sunset view, past the Green Mubazzarah hot springs.

Questions

How much is the private Al Ain Garden City Tour?

From AED 1,150 per car for up to 10 hours: Business Sedan AED 1,150; Family SUV AED 1,390; First Class AED 2,090; Business Van AED 2,290; Luxury SUV AED 2,590. Tolls and parking are included; entrance tickets are not.

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car, for the whole tour. A Business Sedan takes up to 3 guests, a Family SUV up to 6, and a Business Van or Luxury SUV up to 7.

Is a tour guide included?

No. Your chauffeur drives you and waits at every stop, and can suggest places, but he is not a licensed guide. We can add a licensed guide on request at extra cost.

What is not included?

Entrance tickets, food and drinks, optional activities and a licensed tour guide (your chauffeur drives; he is not a guide).

Can I change the stops?

Yes. It is a private tour, so you can skip stops, add others or spend longer where you like, as long as it fits in the booked hours.

What if the tour runs longer?

Extra time is charged at the hourly price of your vehicle class, from AED 150 per hour for a Business Sedan.

Can you pick us up outside Dubai?

Yes. Pickups in Sharjah, Ajman, Abu Dhabi and other emirates are possible; tell us your address and we confirm the price.
