Included in the price

FAQ

Fleet questions

Will I get the exact car I book?

You book a vehicle class, and you can name the car you prefer. We confirm that car when it is free; otherwise we send another car from the same class.

Are the prices per car?

Yes. Trip prices are per car, one way, and hourly prices are per car per hour (minimum 3 hours).

What is included?

Salik and Darb tolls, airport parking, child seats and 45 minutes of free waiting at airports (15 minutes elsewhere).

Do you have electric cars?

Yes: the Tesla Model Y, BYD Han and BYD Song+ are electric, and the Lexus ES 300h is a hybrid.
