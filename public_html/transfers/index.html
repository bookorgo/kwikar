FAQ

Transfer questions

How much is a private transfer from Dubai Airport?

From AED 120 per car to Deira and Bur Dubai, and from AED 150 to Downtown Dubai. See every route above.

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car, one way. A Business Sedan takes up to 3 guests, a Family SUV up to 6 and a Business Van up to 7.

What is included in the price?

Salik and Darb tolls, airport parking, child seats and 45 minutes of free waiting at airports (15 minutes elsewhere).

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book. The driver follows the flight; the first 45 minutes after landing are free.

Do I get the exact car model?

You book a vehicle class. Our team sends the car from that class that is free at your time, for example a Mercedes E-Class or a Lexus ES for a Business Sedan.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Tell us as soon as possible. We confirm the change and cancellation terms for your ride before you pay.
