Morning vs Evening Desert Safari in Dubai: Which Should You Book?

By the KWIKAR team · Published 30 Oct 2026 · Updated 30 Oct 2026

Book an evening safari if you want the full experience: dune bashing, sunset, a camel ride, dinner and live shows at a desert camp, in about 6–8 hours. Book a morning safari if you want the dunes without the long evening: about 4 hours of dune bashing and photos in a private car, with no meal or show.

Evening safari
Dunes, sunset, camel ride, dinner and shows
Morning safari
Dunes and photos, no meal or show

First visit, families, a full night out

The short answer

Most first-time visitors choose the evening desert safari, because it packs the whole desert experience into one trip: the drive over the dunes, the sunset, and dinner with live shows at a camp. The morning desert safari is for people who want the thrill of the dunes and the photos, then the rest of the day free.

Prices come from live supplier rates. The morning safari is priced per private car for up to 6 people, so it works out cheaper per person for a group.

Choose the evening safari if…

  • It is your first time in the desert and you want the full experience in one go.
  • You want sunset photos on the dunes.
  • You are travelling with family and want dinner and entertainment included.
  • You are happy to spend the afternoon and evening on it.

Choose the morning safari if…

  • You have a busy evening or limited time in Dubai.
  • You mainly want dune bashing and photos, not a camp dinner.
  • You are a group of up to 6 and prefer your own car.
  • You are visiting in the cooler months, when mornings in the desert are pleasant.

Good to know for both

  • Dune bashing is bumpy. It is not suitable for pregnant women or guests with back, neck or heart conditions.
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off in Dubai are included on both.
  • Quad bikes and buggies can be added for an extra fee.
  • From May to September the desert is very hot in the middle of the day; evening safaris start in the afternoon and finish after dark.

Questions

Which is better, a morning or evening desert safari?

For most first-time visitors, the evening safari is better because it includes the sunset, dinner and live shows. The morning safari suits people who want dune bashing and photos in a shorter trip.

How long is a morning desert safari?

About 4 hours including hotel pickup and drop-off.

Is food included on the morning safari?

No. The morning safari covers dune bashing and photo stops; meals, the camp and shows are part of the evening safari.

Can children go on a desert safari?

Yes, on most safaris, but dune bashing is not recommended for very young children. Ask us about the best option for your family.
