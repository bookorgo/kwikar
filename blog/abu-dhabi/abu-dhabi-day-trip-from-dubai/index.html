Abu Dhabi Day Trip from Dubai: What to See and How to Get There

By the KWIKAR team · Published 7 Nov 2026 · Updated 7 Nov 2026

Abu Dhabi is about 140 km from Dubai, a drive of around 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes, so a day trip is easy. Most visitors start with the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, then add Qasr Al Watan, the Corniche or the Louvre Abu Dhabi, or spend the whole day at the Yas Island theme parks. A shared guided city tour is the simplest option; a private car lets you set your own plan. Dress modestly for the mosque and remember that it closes around Friday midday prayers.

Distance
About 140 km; 1 h 15–1 h 30 by car each way
Time to allow

Is a day trip to Abu Dhabi worth it?

Yes, especially if you have four days or more in Dubai. Abu Dhabi, the UAE's capital, feels calmer and greener than Dubai, and its best-known sights are different: a vast white mosque, a working presidential palace, a museum of world art and a theme park island. A full day is enough to see the main landmarks without rushing, and you are back in Dubai for dinner.

If you only have one or two days in Dubai, stay in the city and see our 3-day Dubai itinerary first. Abu Dhabi suits a longer trip or a second visit.

The main sights

  • Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: the highlight for most visitors, with white marble courtyards, domes and huge prayer halls. Allow about 90 minutes. Visitors pre-book an online access pass on the mosque's official website, and free guided walking tours run daily in English and Arabic.
  • Qasr Al Watan: the presidential palace, open to visitors, with grand halls, calligraphy and exhibits on how the country is governed. A Qasr Al Watan ticket is open-dated. The palace sometimes closes at short notice for royal occasions, so our team checks before your visit.
  • Louvre Abu Dhabi: art from around the world under a large domed roof on Saadiyat Island. It opens from 10am and is closed every Monday. Plan two to three hours; see our Louvre Abu Dhabi tickets page for the details.
  • The Corniche, Emirates Palace and Etihad Towers: the waterfront drive with the city's best-known skyline views. Most tours pass these as a drive-through or short photo stop.
  • Heritage Village and the Dates Market: short stops for a look at traditional life and to taste and buy local dates.
  • Yas Island: home to Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World, Yas Waterworld and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi. A theme park visit takes the whole day, so do it as a separate trip from the city sights.

Saadiyat Island also has newer museums such as Zayed National Museum, the Natural History Museum and teamLab Phenomena if you would rather spend the afternoon indoors.

What a guided city tour looks like

The shared English-speaking tour follows this order, according to the supplier's itinerary:

  1. 8am–9am: pick-up from your hotel in Dubai.
  2. Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: about 90 minutes.
  3. Heritage Village: about 25 minutes.
  4. Presidential palace: photo stop outside Qasr Al Watan.
  5. Lunch stop: about an hour. Ask our team whether lunch is included in your option.
  6. Etihad Towers, Emirates Palace and the Corniche: drive-through.
  7. Dates Market: about 20 minutes.
  8. 5pm–6pm: drop-off back in Dubai.

The mosque visit can be changed or shortened during Islamic holidays, when entry is not guaranteed.

Three simple day plans

  • Classic first visit: join the guided city tour. You see the mosque, the palace from outside, the Corniche and the markets without planning anything.
  • Culture day (not Monday): with a private car, start at the Grand Mosque at 9am, go inside Qasr Al Watan late morning, then spend the afternoon at the Louvre Abu Dhabi on Saadiyat Island.
  • Theme park day: go straight to Yas Island and spend the day at one or two parks. A Yas Island multi-park ticket covers two parks in one day, or more parks over several days.

Grand Mosque rules to know

  • Dress: long, loose, ankle-length clothes for men and women. Women must cover their hair with a headscarf. Bring your own scarf and cover-up.
  • Friday: the mosque is open in the morning, closes around midday prayers and reopens at 2:30pm.
  • Food and drink: not allowed inside, not even water, and smoking is not allowed anywhere on the grounds.
  • Photos: allowed for personal use at the marked photo stops.
  • Children: welcome, but they must stay with their parents throughout the visit.

Tips for the day

  • Leave early: morning traffic leaving Dubai can add time, and the mosque is calmer soon after it opens.
  • Check the day of the week: the Louvre is closed on Mondays, and the mosque closes around Friday midday prayers.
  • Keep the theme parks separate: Yas Island and the city sights are both full-day plans.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: the mosque courtyards and museums involve a lot of walking.
  • Send your request early in busy weeks: tours and cars fill up around public holidays and big events.

Questions

How far is Abu Dhabi from Dubai?

About 140 km. By car the drive takes around 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes each way, depending on where you start and the traffic.

Can you do Abu Dhabi in one day from Dubai?

Yes. A full day, roughly 8am to 6pm, covers the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Corniche, a palace stop and the markets. The Yas Island theme parks need a separate full day.

What should I wear to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque?

Long, loose, ankle-length clothes for both men and women. Women must also wear a headscarf. Bring your own scarf and cover-up.

Is the Grand Mosque open on Friday?

Yes, but not all day. It opens in the morning, closes around midday prayers and reopens at 2:30pm. Plan a Friday visit for the morning or the afternoon.

Is the Louvre Abu Dhabi open every day?

No. The Louvre Abu Dhabi is closed every Monday. On other days it opens from 10am.

Is a guided tour or a private car better for an Abu Dhabi day trip?

A shared guided tour is the easiest choice for first-time visitors and small groups. A private car suits families and groups who want their own plan, since the price is per car rather than per person.
