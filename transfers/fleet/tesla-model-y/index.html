Questions

How much is a Tesla Model Y with driver in Dubai?

Transfers start at AED 140 per car, one way, and hourly hire costs AED 180 per hour with a minimum of 3 hours. Prices include Salik and Darb tolls, airport parking, child seats and 45 minutes of free waiting at airports (15 minutes elsewhere).

Will I get exactly this Tesla Model Y?

You book the Family SUV class and write that you prefer the Tesla Model Y. We confirm the Tesla Model Y when it is free; otherwise we send another car from the same class such as the BYD Song+ Flagship.

How many people and bags fit in the Tesla Model Y?

Up to 4 guests and about 3–4 suitcases. Tell us your luggage when you book and we confirm the right car.

Is the photo the exact car?

The photo shows this model. The car sent for your booking may differ in colour, year or details.

How do I book?

Send your route, date, time and guests with the form or on WhatsApp. Our team confirms the car and the final price before you pay. There is no online payment.
