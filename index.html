Tips for Visiting Dubai and Abu Dhabi

  • Plan around the heat. Summer days are very hot, so book desert and outdoor activities for early morning or evening. Indoor options such as Ski Dubai and the aquarium suit midday.
  • Dress modestly at religious and cultural sites. Shoulders and knees should be covered at mosques, and women need a headscarf at the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.
  • Check seasonal openings. Outdoor attractions such as Global Village and Dubai Miracle Garden open for a season, not all year.
  • Respect Ramadan customs. During the holy month, avoid eating or drinking in public during daylight hours.

Can KWIKAR help me get a UAE tourist visa?

Yes. We assist with 14, 30 and 60-day UAE tourist visas, single and multiple entry, from AED 400. Processing usually takes three to four working days. You typically need a passport valid for six months and a recent colour photo. The issuing authority makes the final decision.
