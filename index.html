LOCAL TIPS

Tips for visiting Dubai and Abu Dhabi

☀

Plan around the heat

Summer days are very hot. Book desert and outdoor activities for early morning or evening; Ski Dubai and the aquarium suit midday.

✦

Dress modestly at cultural sites

Cover shoulders and knees at mosques. Women need a headscarf at the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

◷

Check seasonal openings

Outdoor attractions such as Global Village and Dubai Miracle Garden open for a season, roughly October to May.

☾

Respect Ramadan customs

During the holy month, avoid eating or drinking in public during daylight hours.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a tour or activity with KWIKAR?

Choose an experience on our website and send a request with your preferred date and number of guests. You can also message us on WhatsApp or call +971 50 559 9243. Our team checks availability with the supplier, confirms the final price and booking terms, and only then asks for payment.

Are the prices on KWIKAR final?

Prices shown are starting prices, and tour prices come from live supplier rates. The final amount depends on your date, group size, ticket type and any extras. We send you a confirmed quote before you pay, so you know the full cost in advance.

Does KWIKAR offer airport transfers from Dubai Airport?

Yes. We arrange private transfers from Dubai International Airport (DXB) to hotels in Downtown, DIFC, Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah and other areas. A Mercedes E-Class to Downtown or DIFC starts from AED 150. Share your flight number so we can plan the pickup.

Can KWIKAR help me get a UAE tourist visa?

Yes. We assist with 14, 30 and 60-day UAE tourist visas, single and multiple entry, from AED 400. Processing usually takes three to four working days. You typically need a passport valid for six months and a recent colour photo. The issuing authority makes the final decision.

Which UAE cities does KWIKAR cover?

Our UAE destinations are Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Ajman. Private chauffeur transfers also run between emirates, for example from Dubai to Abu Dhabi. For travel outside the UAE, we arrange holiday packages to several countries in Asia and Europe.

Do you offer holiday packages for families and couples?

Yes. Options include multi-night Dubai and Abu Dhabi packages, and Singapore trips such as Gardens to Galaxies: A Family Voyage in Singapore and Sweet Escapes: Honeymoon Singapore Getaway. Each package is priced for your travel dates and preferred hotel. Send us your plans and we will share a tailored quote.
