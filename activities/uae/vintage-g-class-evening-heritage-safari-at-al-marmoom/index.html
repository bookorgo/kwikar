Vintage G-Class Evening Heritage Safari at Al Marmoom

Al Marmoom Desert Conservation Reserve · Hotel pickup in Dubai · About 6 hours

Hotel pickup in Dubai includedNo dune bashingBook on WhatsApp

The Vintage G-Class Heritage Safari is a calm evening desert tour in the Al Marmoom Desert Conservation Reserve, south of Dubai. You ride for 30–40 minutes in an open-top classic Mercedes G-Class, then join a camel caravan, meet a falcon, have a five-course Bedouin set dinner and finish with stargazing at the telescopes. Hotel pickup and drop-off in Dubai are included, the tour takes about 6 hours, and there is no dune bashing. Prices start from AED 690 per person on the shared tour.

Overview

Most evening desert safaris in Dubai are built around dune bashing in a modern 4x4 and a busy camp with shows. This one is slower and quieter. It takes place in the Al Marmoom Desert Conservation Reserve, an unfenced protected area set up in January 2018 that covers about a tenth of Dubai's land area and includes the Al Qudra Lakes.

The highlight is the drive itself: 30–40 minutes across the reserve in an open-top classic Mercedes G-Class, with a guide who points out desert plants and wildlife on the way. The rest of the evening is about Bedouin heritage: Arabic coffee and dates on arrival, a camel caravan at sunset, a falcon you can hold for a photo, henna painting, a five-course Bedouin set dinner and a telescope session to end the night.

The supplier says the dinner menu comes from Al Khayma Heritage Restaurant, which holds a Bib Gourmand in the 2026 MICHELIN Guide Dubai for its Emirati cooking in Al Fahidi. If you want more adrenaline, compare the evening desert safari with dinner and live shows, or read our guide to morning vs evening desert safari.

Best for

  • Couples: A quiet desert evening with dinner under the stars, without the noise of a large camp.
  • Classic car and photo lovers: Open-top vintage G-Class, camels and sunset light in one evening.
  • Guests who skip dune bashing: The drive is a gentle ride through the reserve, not a dune-bashing session.

About this experience

How much is the vintage G-Class safari in Dubai?

On KWIKAR the shared tour starts from AED 690 per person, with a lower child rate. The private car is one price for up to 6 guests. The current price of each option is shown above, and our team confirms the final price before you pay.

Where is Al Marmoom?

The Al Marmoom Desert Conservation Reserve is inland, south of the city towards Al Qudra. It is an unfenced protected area covering about a tenth of Dubai's land area. Hotel pickup and drop-off in Dubai are included, so you do not need to find it yourself.

What time does the evening G-Class safari start?

Pickup on the shared tour is from about 2pm, and the tour takes about 6 hours including the transfers. Times change with the season and your hotel, so our team confirms the exact pickup time before the day.

Is there dune bashing?

No. The vintage G-Class drive is a 30–40 minute ride through the reserve in an open-top classic car. If you want dune bashing, choose an evening desert safari in a modern 4x4 instead.

What is included in the dinner?

A five-course Bedouin set dinner, with Arabic coffee, tea, dates, unlimited water and soft drinks. Tell us about dietary needs when you send your request.

Can children join?

Yes. The activities are gentle. On the shared tour, children aged 4–11 pay the child rate and under-4s join free, and children under 12 ride the camels with an adult. Send the children's ages and our team confirms the right ticket for each.

Who should not do this tour?

Pregnant guests, people with back or neck problems and wheelchair users, because of the off-road drive and the camel ride.

What should I wear?

Casual clothes and closed shoes. Bring a warm layer for winter evenings (about November to March) and sunglasses for the open-top drive.

Shared tour or private car: which should I book?

The shared tour is priced per person and suits couples and small groups. The private car is one price for up to 6 guests and keeps the evening to your own group.

Can I drive the G-Class?

No. A licensed driver-guide drives the vehicle and explains the reserve on the way.
