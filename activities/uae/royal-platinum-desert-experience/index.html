Royal Platinum Desert Experience

UAE · Desert Tours & Activities

You can book Royal Platinum Desert Experience in UAE with KWIKAR from AED 980 per person. There are 2 options to choose from. Send your preferred date and number of guests on WhatsApp; our team confirms availability and the final price before you pay.

  • LocationUAE
  • Duration7 hours
  • PriceFrom AED 980 per person (up to AED 13,320)
  • Options2 ticket or tour options
  • ChildrenChild prices available on some options
  • Runs onEvery day

Overview

The most exclusive desert evening in Dubai. A private Land Rover Defender collects you in the afternoon, your Conservation Guide drives you through the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve, and you arrive at a private oasis set up only for your group.

Sunset canapés and sparkling juice come with a private falcon show. Then a personal chef prepares a five-course menu by a Michelin-starred chef, served by your own butler among fire torches and a quiet lake. A fire show ends the night, and you can add a violinist, a saxophonist or acrobats.

What to expect

  • Private nature drive: 60 minutes in a Land Rover Defender, with photos next to a fleet of museum-quality vintage Land Rovers and at a remote lake.
  • Private oasis: your own camp: Nestled Platinum Oasis for 2-10 guests or Royal Platinum Oasis for 8 or more.
  • Private falcon show: at sunset, with canapés and sparkling juice.
  • Five-course fine dining: a Michelin-starred chef's menu with caviar, cooked on site and served by your butler.
  • Fire show: a dramatic performance to end the evening; shisha or cigars in the majlis if you wish.

Perfect for

  • Proposals and anniversaries: a romantic, completely private setting.
  • VIP and corporate groups: private for 2 to 20 guests, with entertainment you can add.
  • Luxury travellers: the highest level of service in the Dubai desert.

How it works

  1. Private pick-up in Dubai

    Your Land Rover Defender collects you between 14:30 and 16:30, depending on the season.

  2. Nature drive and lake

    A private 60-minute drive through the reserve and photos at a remote lake.

  3. Sunset at your private oasis

    Canapés, sparkling juice and a private falcon show.

  4. Five-course dinner

    A Michelin-starred chef's menu, served by your butler.

  5. Fire show

    A fire performance under the stars to end the night.

  6. Return to Dubai

    Drop-off between 21:30 and 23:30. The experience lasts about seven hours.

Questions

About this experience

Is everything private?

Yes. The car, the guide, the camp, the chef and the butler are only for your group.

Can we add entertainment?

Yes. Ask for a violinist, a saxophonist or an acrobatic show when you book.

How many guests can join?

2 to 20 guests. For bigger groups, contact us.

Can you cater for special diets?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and kosher-style meals are possible. Tell us about diets and allergies when you book.

Who should not join the nature drive?

The drive is not recommended for guests who are more than six months pregnant.

Booking with KWIKAR

How do I book this with KWIKAR?

Tap Check availability, choose your date, guests and option, and send the request on WhatsApp. Our team checks availability with the supplier and replies with the final AED price before you pay.

Are there booking or card fees?

No. The price our team confirms on WhatsApp is the total you pay. KWIKAR does not add a separate booking or card fee.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Changes and refunds follow the supplier's rules for this experience, shown in the cancellation policy on this page. Contact us as soon as possible and we will check what applies to your booking.
