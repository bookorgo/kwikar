Overview

Falcons have been part of Emirati life for centuries. On this private morning experience a professional falconer shows you how falcons, hawks and owls are trained, and how the birds hunt with ancient and modern techniques.

During the 75-minute interactive session a bird lands on your glove, a moment of real trust between you and the falcon. Then a luxurious Land Rover Defender takes you on a nature drive through the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve, past a remote lake, to a gourmet breakfast at the famous Al Maha Desert Resort & Spa.

What to expect

  • Private falconry session: 75 minutes with an expert falconer in a desert camp, with falcons, hawks and owls.
  • Bird on your glove: hold a bird of prey and learn how it is cared for and trained.
  • Ethical approach: the birds' health and welfare always come first.
  • Nature drive: 60 minutes in a Land Rover Defender with a Conservation Guide, with a stop at a remote lake.
  • Breakfast at Al Maha: a chef's breakfast menu on the deck of the resort.

Perfect for

  • Animal lovers: a hands-on, ethical encounter with birds of prey.
  • Private groups: the whole experience is only for your group.
  • Culture seekers: falconry is a UNESCO-listed tradition and a symbol of the UAE.

Questions

About this experience

Can children join?

Yes, but the Al Maha breakfast is for children aged 10 and over. Ask us about younger children before you book.

Will I hold a bird?

Yes. The falconer places a bird on your glove during the session.

When does it operate?

Every morning from 1 November to 30 April.

What should I wear?

Light, comfortable clothes, a hat, sunglasses and sunscreen. From November to March bring a warm layer for the evening and the early morning.

Who should not join the nature drive?

The drive is not recommended for guests who are more than six months pregnant.

Can you cater for special diets?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and kosher-style meals are possible. Tell us about diets and allergies when you book.
