Heritage Morning Safari: Bedouin Culture

UAE · Desert Tours & Activities

You can book Heritage Morning Safari: Bedouin Culture in UAE with KWIKAR. There are 5 options to choose from. Send your preferred date and number of guests on WhatsApp; our team confirms availability and the final price before you pay.

  • LocationUAE
  • Duration5 hours
  • Options5 ticket or tour options
  • Runs onEvery day

Overview

Start the day the way the Bedouin did. On this morning safari in the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve you travel by camel to a traditional village, meet the falconer and his birds, and sit down to a real Emirati breakfast.

Conservation Guides tell the story of desert life and heritage, young locals invite you to join a traditional dance, and the morning ends with a 60-minute nature drive in a vintage Land Rover, the first motor vehicle in the UAE. You are back at your hotel before lunch.

What to expect

  • Camel caravan: a 15-minute ride to the Bedouin village; two guests share each camel.
  • Bedouin village: tents, cooking stations and farm animals, and a live Arabic coffee demonstration.
  • Falcon encounter: meet a falcon up close, talk to the falconer and take photos.
  • Emirati breakfast: Balaleet, Chabab pancakes, Ragag bread, local cheese with dates and more.
  • Al Rafza dance: join young locals in a traditional celebratory performance.
  • Vintage Land Rover drive: 60 minutes through the reserve looking for oryx and gazelles.

Perfect for

  • Families: gentle, hands-on activities, with a child rate for ages 5-11 and a family pass for 2 adults and 2 children.
  • Early risers: cool morning air, soft light for photos and the afternoon free.
  • Culture lovers: an authentic look at Emirati traditions, food and music.

How it works

  1. Hotel pick-up in Dubai

    Pick-up between 05:30 and 06:30, depending on the season. A headscarf and a water bottle are your gifts.

  2. Camel caravan to the village

    A 15-minute camel ride across the dunes to a Bedouin village.

  3. Falcon encounter

    See a falcon up close and take photos with the falconer.

  4. Emirati breakfast

    A traditional breakfast, with a live coffee demonstration and a Bedouin dance.

  5. Vintage Land Rover drive

    60 minutes through the reserve with your Conservation Guide.

  6. Return to Dubai

    Drop-off between 10:30 and 12:00. The tour lasts about five hours.

Questions

About this experience

How early is the pick-up?

Between 05:30 and 06:30, depending on the season. You get the exact time the afternoon before.

Can children join?

Yes. Children 5-11 pay the child rate, and a family pass covers 2 adults and 2 children. Children under 5 need a private car.

How long is the camel ride?

About 15 minutes. Two guests share one camel.

When does it operate?

Every day from 1 November to 30 April.

Who should not join the nature drive?

The drive is not recommended for guests who are more than six months pregnant.

Does the tour support conservation?

Yes. A part of every booking goes to conservation work in the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve.
