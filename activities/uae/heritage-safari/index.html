Overview

We invite you to experience the authentic traditions of the Emiratis and embark on a journey "back in time" on retro 1950s Land Rovers in the largest ecological park in Dubai, Al Marmoom.

Here you can observe wild sand gazelles and white oryx antelopes grazing in their natural desert habitat. 

To enhance the desert experience, you will have the opportunity to take a camel ride, also known as the "ships of the desert", near a Bedouin camp. Dinner will be served in traditional local style with authentic dishes and beverages.

Itinerary

Arrival at Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve

Enter the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve, a protected area that covers 225 square kilometers of the country's desert ecosystem. This reserve is a testament to Dubai's commitment to preserving its unique environment. Here, you'll witness a pristine stretch of desert landscape, home to many species of wildlife and plants.

60-minute Nature Safari

Board a 4x4 vehicle driven by an experienced desert guide. Over the next hour, traverse the undulating dunes and valleys, known as "wadis". Keep an eye out for native wildlife like the Arabian Oryx, gazelles, and various bird species. Your guide will share insights about the desert's ecology, the survival techniques of its inhabitants, and the history of the region.

Falconry Show at Sunset

As the sun begins to set, casting a golden hue over the dunes, gather to witness the ancient art of falconry. Watch in awe as these magnificent birds of prey demonstrate their hunting skills, diving and swooping at incredible speeds. Learn about the significance of falconry in Bedouin culture and its importance in the UAE's heritage.

Transfer to the Camp

After the falconry show, you'll be transferred to a traditional desert camp. The camp is designed to replicate the Bedouin lifestyle, complete with tents, cushions, and carpets.

Camel Riding

Experience the desert as the Bedouins have for centuries - on the back of a camel. These 'ships of the desert' offer a unique vantage point, allowing you to feel the rhythm of the desert and connect with its vastness.

Traditional Bread and Coffee Making Demonstration

Discover the culinary traditions of the Bedouin people. Participate in a demonstration on how to make traditional Arabic bread and coffee. Taste the freshly made bread, and sip on the aromatic coffee, known as "Gahwa", often flavored with cardamom.

Traditional 4-course Dinner

As the evening progresses, indulge in a sumptuous 4-course dinner. The menu typically includes a variety of salads, grilled meats, traditional dishes like hummus and tabbouleh, and delectable desserts. Enjoy your meal under the starlit desert sky.

Cultural Show

Post-dinner, immerse yourself in the rich cultural performances of the region. The evening's entertainment includes an authentic drumming performance, setting the rhythm for the night. This is followed by the Yola dance, a traditional Emirati dance that involves performers twirling long sticks, showcasing their agility and coordination.
