UAE · Water Based Activities

You can book Ocean Empress Dinner Cruise in UAE with KWIKAR from AED 149 per person. There are 6 options to choose from. Send your preferred date and number of guests on WhatsApp; our team confirms availability and the final price before you pay.

  • Duration2 hours
  • PriceFrom AED 149 per person (up to AED 399)

Overview

Indulge in a luxurious dinner onboard Dubai’s richest and largest boat, the Ocean Empress Dhow Dinner Cruise. The 16,000 square feet boat offers four decks, each offering spectacular views of the coastline, and can accommodate up to 500 people
