Overview

Fujairah's east coast reveals a different side of the UAE — one where the rugged Hajar Mountains meet the turquoise waters of the Gulf of Oman, far from the glass towers of Dubai.

Here centuries-old heritage sits quietly beside the present day: an ancient mosque still used for prayer, a fortress that once guarded mountain trade routes, and an elegant modern mosque rising nearby as a symbol of the emirate today.

What awaits you

  • Al Bidya Mosque: the UAE's oldest mosque — four domes resting on a single internal column, preserved since the 15th century.
  • Khorfakkan Waterfall: a picturesque man-made waterfall by the corniche amphitheatre, a popular photo spot.
  • Al Rafisah Dam: an artificial lake framed by the Hajar Mountains, ideal for scenic photos.
  • Fujairah Fort: a historic fortress with massive walls, telling the story of the emirate's past.
  • Sheikh Zayed Mosque: an elegant modern complex and one of Fujairah's landmark buildings.

Perfect for

  • Nature and photography lovers: mountains, a lake and a waterfall in one route.
  • History and culture lovers: the UAE's oldest mosque and a centuries-old fort.
  • First-time visitors to the east coast: Fujairah's main sights covered in a single day.
