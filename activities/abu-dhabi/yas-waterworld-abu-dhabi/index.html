Overview

Dive into a world of splash-tastic adventure at Yas Waterworld, the Middle East’s iconic waterpark destination located on Yas Island, Abu Dhabi. Boasting over 40 exhilarating rides, slides, and attractions, Yas Waterworld promises adrenaline-fueled thrills, family-friendly fun, and one-of-a-kind experiences. Whether you’re plunging down high-speed slides like the legendary Jebel Drop, rafting through the curving channels of Falcon’s Falaj, or conquering the waves in the enormous surf pool, every corner of the park brims with excitement.

Beyond the rides, immerse yourself in the rich Emirati-inspired story of “The Legend of the Lost Pearl,” woven throughout the park’s vibrant theme. Experience a taste of local heritage with pearl diving shows or relax on sun-kissed loungers by calm, lazy rivers. Little ones can delight in dedicated splash areas crafted just for them, while thrill-seekers scale new heights of excitement on record-breaking slides. With attentive lifeguards, diverse dining options, and plenty of shaded seating areas, Yas Waterworld delivers comfort, safety, and unforgettable memories all in one place.

Come and discover why Yas Waterworld is more than just a waterpark—it’s an immersive journey where families and friends can bond over shared moments of laughter, thrills, and pure aquatic delight. Book your tickets now and get ready to make waves at the region’s most spectacular water adventure!

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

The recommended dresscode is beachwear. You must have swimwear and towels. There is an opportunity to buy swimwear in the water park stores, also there is a towel rental.

What are the opening hours?

Opening hours are 10am-6pm. The Time spent in the water park - 5 hours.

Can I bring water and food?

You can bring 0.5 liters of water into the park, food is prohibited. There are cafes in the park, where you can buy water and snacks.

What is the child policy?

Children under 3 years old free of charge (without providing a seat on the bus for the group tour).  Children under 1.1m - children's ticket.  Children above 1.1m - adult ticket. Documents confirming the age of the child may be required at the entrance. To visit some slides there are restrictions on height.  For small children is equipped with a special area for playing and bathing. All young children must wear armbands and diapers for swimming.

Fast track admission to attractions?

You can purchase a Quick Pass from our representative.
