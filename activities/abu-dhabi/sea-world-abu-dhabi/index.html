Overview

Get ready to explore an exciting world that will allow you to go on an incredible journey from poles to tropics in SeaWorld Abu Dhabi theme park. Here you will meet the inhabitants almost from all over the world oceans.

SeaWorld Abu Dhabi offeres more than 35 interactive attractions, 17 restaurants, shops and other entertainment. There are live performances of fun characters for the guests of the oceanarium.

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

Casual but modest clothing is recommended.

Can I bring water to the park?

You can bring 0.5 liters of water to the park, bringing food to the park is forbidden.

Child policy

Children under 3 years old go free. Children under 1.1 m - child ticket Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Documents confirming the age of the child may be required at the entrance.
