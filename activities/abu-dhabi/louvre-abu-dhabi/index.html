Overview

Step into the Louvre Abu Dhabi, the Arab world’s first universal museum and a symbol of cultural dialogue and exchange. Located in the Saadiyat Cultural District, this landmark institution showcases a curated journey through humanity’s artistic heritage, spanning ancient civilizations to modern innovation.

With 9,200 square metres of display space, the museum’s permanent collection is enriched by loans from renowned French institutions such as the Musée du Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, and Centre Pompidou. Temporary exhibitions add fresh perspectives throughout the year. Visitors are invited to explore the shared threads between global cultures, presented in a way that transcends borders, timelines, and traditions.

Designed by acclaimed architect Jean Nouvel, the museum is a work of art in itself. Inspired by traditional Arabic architecture, the building is crowned with a monumental dome composed of 7,850 interlaced stars. This floating canopy filters sunlight to create a stunning “rain of light,” casting intricate shadows across the open spaces below.

Surrounded by the waters of the Arabian Gulf, Louvre Abu Dhabi offers a serene and immersive environment to admire artistic treasures from across the world. From ancient relics to contemporary masterpieces, the museum celebrates the richness of human creativity across time and place.

Temporary Exhibitions

Each year, the museum hosts several temporary exhibitions created in collaboration with its French partner museums, as well as regional and international cultural institutions.

Children's Museum at Louvre Abu Dhabi

A world of games, where board games open the door to adventure, imagination and connection. This children's exhibition explores how chess, mancala, ludo and carrom have brought people and cultures together for centuries.

Questions

About this experience

Dress code

Dress modestly, ensuring that shoulders and knees are covered. Comfortable footwear is recommended, as the visit involves considerable walking. It is also recommended to bring a light jacket or outerwear, as the museum is air-conditioned.

Experience duration

The average visit duration is approximately 2 to 3 hours, depending on the number of visitors.

Child Policy

Children under 18 years old – free of charge. Visitors aged 18 and above – adult rate applies. Children under the age of 13 must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or older. Proof of the child's age may be required.

Where is the Louvre Abu Dhabi located?

Louvre Abu Dhabi is situated on Saadiyat Island, within the Saadiyat Cultural District in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

Do I need to print the ticket?

Both printed and electronic confirmations are accepted.

Required Documents

A valid ticket (either printed or electronic) is required for entry.

Are guests permitted to bring outside food and beverages?

Visitors are not allowed to bring food and beverages.
