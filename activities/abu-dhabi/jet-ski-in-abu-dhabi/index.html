Overview

Highlights

  • Ride along the world-famous Corniche, Abu Dhabi's sweeping waterfront promenade
  • Cruise past the Emirates Palace, one of the most luxurious hotels on the planet
  • Explore the waters around Al Lulu Island and take in the city's best coastal views
  • Stop for photo opportunities with Abu Dhabi's skyline as your backdrop
  • Command your own powerful jet ski — no experience necessary

What to Expect

This isn't a passive sightseeing tour. You'll take the helm of your own jet ski and cut through the calm, turquoise waters of the Arabian Gulf, with Abu Dhabi's most iconic landmarks lining the shore. Whether you're in it for the speed or the scenery — or both — this experience delivers an unforgettable perspective on the capital.

Perfect For

  • Thrill-seekers and adventure lovers
  • First-time visitors wanting a unique way to explore Abu Dhabi
  • Photographers looking for iconic waterfront backdrops

Questions

About this experience

What should I bring for water sport session?

You only need your ID and swimwear.

Do I need any experience to ride?

No experience is required. Our instructors guide you step by step to ensure a safe and enjoyable ride.

How early should I arrive before my booking?

We recommend arriving 15–20 minutes earlier to complete registration and receive safety briefing.

Is photo and video included?

Yes. All Jet Ski sessions include drone footage or GoPro photos, depending on weather and regulations. You can choose the package on the spot for extra charge.

Age policy

The minimum age for jet ski riding is 16 years; however, guests aged 6 years and older can ride as passengers.

Safety precautions

All equipment is maintained to the highest marine standards, and every session is supervised by certified professionals.
