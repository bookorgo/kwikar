Overview

Experience the Formula 1 Etihad Airways Abu Dhabi Grand Prix 2026 at Yas Marina Circuit from 3–6 December. Watch the season finale under the lights from a grandstand, the Abu Dhabi Hill or a hospitality suite. Every ticket includes general admission to the Yasalam presented by e& After-Race Concerts at Etihad Park, access to the F1® Fanzone, 1-day entry to teamLab Phenomena Abu Dhabi or one Yas Island theme park, and entry to Louvre Abu Dhabi and Qasr Al Watan.
