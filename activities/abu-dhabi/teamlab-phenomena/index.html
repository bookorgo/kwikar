Overview

TeamLab Phenomena in Abu Dhabi is an immersive art experience that showcases the innovative work of the Tokyo-based art collective, teamLab. The exhibition, which opened in 2023, is part of the broader vision to create a cultural hub in the UAE, blending art with technology to engage audiences in unique and interactive ways.


Spanning over 17,000 square feet, the exhibition features a series of immersive installations that emphasize the relationship between nature and digital art. Visitors are invited to step into a series of vibrant, moving landscapes where the lines between reality and digital art blur. The installations are designed to be experiential and interactive, allowing guests to engage with the art directly. For instance, as participants walk through these spaces, their movements influence the artwork, creating a dynamic experience that changes in real-time.


One of the hallmark features of TeamLab Phenomena is its focus on collaboration and interconnectedness. The artworks often respond to the presence of viewers, encouraging social interaction and collective participation. This aspect speaks to belief in the importance of human connections in a fast-paced digital age, making the exhibition not just a visual feast, but also a community experience.


The exhibitions often draw inspiration from natural elements, such as water, light, and flora, resulting in stunning visual displays that highlight both the beauty of the natural world and the capabilities of advanced technology. The use of projection mapping, sensors, and augmented reality creates immersive environments that captivate the senses, encouraging visitors to explore deeply and interactively.


TeamLab’s philosophy is anchored in transcending traditional boundaries of art by combining science, technology, and art. This approach invites discussions about the future of art and the impact of technology on human experiences. The teamLab collective operates on a concept they call “Borderless,” which emphasizes the idea that art should not have physical boundaries or restrictions.


Abu Dhabi's hosting of TeamLab Phenomena represents a significant addition to its cultural landscape, aligning with the city's vision to promote creativity and innovation. It connects international cultural movements with local audiences, fostering a greater appreciation for contemporary art forms among visitors and residents alike.


Overall, TeamLab Phenomena in Abu Dhabi is not just an art exhibition; it’s a transformative experience that challenges visitors to think about their relationship with art, technology, and one another. It exemplifies how contemporary exhibits can create spaces for reflection, engagement, and togetherness in an increasingly digital world. Whether you’re an art aficionado or a casual visitor, TeamLab Phenomena promises to be an enriching experience that leaves a lasting impression.

About this experience

What is TeamLab Phenomena?

TeamLab Phenomena is an immersive art exhibition created by the Tokyo-based art collective, teamLab. It features a series of interactive, digital artworks that merge technology with nature, allowing visitors to engage with the art through movement and interaction. The exhibition aims to create a unique environment that fosters connections between visitors and the artwork.

Where is TeamLab Phenomena located?

TeamLab Phenomena is located in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. It is part of the city’s efforts to establish a vibrant cultural scene and is accessible to both local residents and international visitors.

How long does it take to experience the exhibition?

The time it takes to experience TeamLab Phenomena can vary based on individual preferences. On average, visitors can spend between one to two hours exploring the various installations and engaging with the interactive elements. Guests are encouraged to take their time to fully appreciate the immersive environment.

Are children allowed in the exhibition?

Yes, TeamLab Phenomena is suitable for visitors of all ages, including children. The interactive nature of the exhibition makes it a fun and engaging experience for families. However, parental supervision is recommended to ensure safety and assistance in navigating the displays.

How can I purchase tickets for TeamLab Phenomena?

Tickets for TeamLab Phenomena can typically be purchased online through the official exhibition website or at the venue's ticketing counter. It is recommended to book tickets in advance, especially during peak visiting times, to ensure availability and avoid long queues.
