Overview

Experience the unparalleled beauty and wonder of the Arabian desert with our exclusive evening safari in Abu Dhabi. As the sun starts its descent, you'll be whisked away in a powerful 4WD vehicle, feeling the adrenaline rush as you glide and weave over golden sand dunes. 

Marvel at the majesty of nature as you stop by a thriving camel farm, where you can observe these gentle giants of the desert in their natural habitat. Gain insight into their significance in the history and culture of the region.

Upon arriving at a traditionally-styled desert camp, you'll be enveloped in the rich tapestry of Arabic culture. Here, you can partake in exhilarating activities such as quad biking, which lets you commandeer your own vehicle over the dunes, or sandboarding, a thrill akin to snowboarding but on the smooth desert sands. For a more serene experience, mount a camel and ride it, feeling its rhythmic gait beneath you as you traverse the desert landscape.

After your adventures, take a moment to dress in authentic Arabic attire. With the setting sun casting golden hues around you, it's the perfect backdrop for a memorable photo shoot. 

As evening deepens, allow your senses to be captivated. Witness the sun painting the vast sky in shades of amber and crimson as it sets, turning the desert into a canvas of breathtaking beauty. The aroma of a sumptuous BBQ buffet dinner will tantalize your senses, offering a delectable range of local and international dishes. 

But the night isn’t over yet. As you dine under the canopy of a starlit sky, you'll be entertained with live performances that capture the soul of Arabic art and culture. Whether it's the mesmerizing swirls of a belly dancer or the haunting melodies of traditional music, you'll be completely immersed in the enchanting ambiance.

Join us for a night that promises not only excitement and adventure but also a deep dive into the rich tapestry of the Arabic desert legacy. An evening you'll reminisce about for years to come awaits you.

About this experience

How long is the evening safari?

The evening safari lasts for approximately 5-6 hours.

Is there an opportunity to ride a camel?

Yes, camel riding is one of the activities included in the safari.

Is the BBQ dinner vegetarian-friendly?

Yes, there are vegetarian options available in the BBQ buffet dinner.

Will I get picked up from the hotel?

Yes, you will be picked up and dropped to the hotel?

What is NOT included?

For the evening safari, the only optionals are dune buggies, quad bikes, sheesha, alcoholic beverages, souvenirs and tips. All of the above is optional.
