Overview

Feel every heartbeat of Los Blancos glory—without flying to Spain. Real Madrid World, inside Dubai Parks & Resorts, is the planet’s first football-club theme park: six hectares (roughly six Bernabéus laid end-to-end) packed with more than 40 rides, shows and exhibits that celebrate 123 years of record-breaking triumphs.
