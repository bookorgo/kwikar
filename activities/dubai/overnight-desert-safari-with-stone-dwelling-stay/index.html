Overview

Spend a full evening and a night in the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve, far from the lights of the city. The afternoon starts with a 60-minute nature drive in a vintage 1950s Land Rover, or a slow camel caravan across the dunes, with a Conservation Guide who explains how plants, animals and the Bedouin survive in the desert.

After a falconry show at sunset you arrive at a torch-lit Bedouin camp for dinner and Emirati entertainment. When the camp goes quiet, you sleep in a traditional stone dwelling room under a sky full of stars, and wake up to a gourmet breakfast before the transfer back to Dubai.

What to expect

  • Nature drive or camel caravan: 60 minutes through the reserve in a vintage Land Rover, or the same journey by camel, with a Conservation Guide.
  • Sunset falconry: a live falcon display against the desert sunset.
  • Bedouin camp: Arabic coffee and dates on arrival, live bread and coffee making, camel rides, henna and shisha.
  • Four-course Emirati dinner: shared starters and traditional mains, with drumming and Yola performances you can join.
  • Night in a stone dwelling: a simple, quiet room with a comfortable bed; the camp power goes off from 23:00 to 06:00.
  • Gourmet breakfast: breakfast at the camp, then the return to Dubai between 09:00 and 10:00.

Perfect for

  • Couples: a peaceful night away from the city, with stargazing and no noise.
  • Families: children aged 5-11 pay the child rate, and the activities are gentle and educational.
  • Culture lovers: a real look at Bedouin life, food and music in an award-winning conservation reserve.

Itinerary

Hotel pick-up in Dubai

Pick-up between 14:00 and 16:30, depending on the season. A headscarf and a steel water bottle are your gifts.

Nature drive or camel caravan

60 minutes through the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve in a vintage Land Rover or by camel.

Sunset falcon show

Watch a falcon fly against the sunset and learn about the art of falconry.

Bedouin camp and dinner

Coffee and dates, bread making, camel rides, then a four-course dinner with Emirati performances.

Night in a stone dwelling

Sleep in a traditional stone room in the quiet of the desert.

Breakfast and return

Gourmet breakfast at the camp and drop-off in Dubai between 09:00 and 10:00.

Questions

About this experience

When does the overnight safari operate?

Every day from 1 November to 30 April.

Can children join?

Yes. Children aged 5-11 pay the child rate. Children under 5 can join only with a private car.

What are the rooms like?

Traditional stone dwelling rooms with a bed and bedding. There is no air-conditioning or heating, and the camp has shared bathrooms but no showers.

Can I bring luggage?

On shared transfers, one small bag of up to 50 cm per guest. For big luggage, book a private car.

What should I wear?

Light, comfortable clothes, a hat, sunglasses and sunscreen. From November to March bring a warm layer for the evening and the early morning.

Can you cater for special diets?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and kosher-style meals are possible. Tell us about diets and allergies when you book.
