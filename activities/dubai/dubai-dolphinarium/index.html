Overview

Get ready to witness the magic of the ocean up close at the Dubai Dolphinarium, one of the most exciting and interactive attractions in Dubai. Located in the heart of the city, the Dolphinarium offers a unique opportunity to get up close and personal with some of the most intelligent and playful creatures of the sea.

Watch in amazement as the highly trained dolphins and seals perform incredible acrobatic feats, showcasing their agility and intelligence in a series of entertaining shows. From spectacular jumps and flips to synchronized swimming and ball games, the performances are sure to leave you mesmerized and delighted.

In addition to the shows, the Dubai Dolphinarium offers a range of interactive experiences, such as swimming with dolphins and feeding them, giving you the chance to get up close and personal with these amazing animals.

Perfect for families and animal lovers, the Dubai Dolphinarium is a must-visit attraction that combines education and entertainment in a fun and interactive way. So why wait? Book your tickets now and get ready to experience the magic of the ocean at the Dubai Dolphinarium.

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

Casual but modest dress code is recommended.

Duration of the show?

The show lasts 45 minutes.

Can I bring water and food?

You can bring 0.5 liters of water into the dolphinarium, food is not allowed. There are cafes in the park, where you can buy water and snacks.

Child Policy?

Children under 2 years old free of charge (without providing a seat on the bus for the group tour).  Children 4-12 years old - children's ticket.  From 12 years - adult ticket. Documents confirming the age of the child may be required at the entrance.
