Overview

The Dubai Frame is a 150-metre structure in Zabeel Park shaped like a giant picture frame. The bridge across the top links two views: the older districts of the city on one side and the modern skyline on the other, so the building frames Dubai's past and present.

A visit has three parts. You start in the Old Dubai gallery, with displays and projections about life before the city grew. A lift then takes you to the Sky Deck, the bridge across the top, with a glass walkway and wide views over the skyline. On the way down, the Future Dubai gallery shows a vision of the city in the decades ahead.

Your KWIKAR ticket is open-dated general admission, so you choose the day that suits you. Hotel transfers are not included, but we can add one. See more attraction tickets or things to do in Dubai.

Best for

  • First-time visitors: One stop that shows old Dubai, the modern skyline and the city's plans for the future.
  • Families: A short, indoor visit with child tickets for ages 3–12 and free entry under 3.
  • Photos: The frame itself makes a striking photo from Zabeel Park, and the Sky Deck gives skyline views in both directions.
  • A short slot in your day: Open from 8am to 9pm, so it fits before or after other sightseeing.
KWIKAR tip

Go late in the afternoon to see the skyline in daylight and again as the city lights come on. Leave strollers and large bags at the Gate 4 counter.

About this experience

How much are Dubai Frame tickets?

On KWIKAR, Dubai Frame tickets start from AED 52.5 per person, with a lower rate for children aged 3 to 12. Our team confirms the final price on WhatsApp before you pay.

What are the Dubai Frame opening hours?

The Dubai Frame is open every day from 8am to 9pm, including weekends and public holidays. Hours may differ during Ramadan and on holidays.

Where is the Dubai Frame and which gate do I use?

It is in Zabeel Park, between Sheikh Zayed Road and Sheikh Rashid Road. The entrance is at Zabeel Park Gate 4.

Which metro station is closest?

Al Jafiliya is the nearest metro station for Zabeel Park.

How tall is the Dubai Frame?

The Dubai Frame is 150 metres tall.

What is inside the Dubai Frame?

The Old Dubai gallery, the Sky Deck bridge at the top with a glass walkway and city views, and the Future Dubai gallery.

Do children need a ticket?

Children under 3 enter free. Children aged 3 to 12 need a child ticket, and under-12s must stay with an adult.

Can I bring a stroller or a large bag?

No. Strollers and large bags must be left at the Gate 4 counter before you go in.

Is the ticket for a fixed date and time?

No. It is an open-dated general admission ticket, so you can visit any day during opening hours. Tell us your planned date when you book.

Is hotel pickup included?

No, the ticket is entry only. We can add a private transfer from your hotel at extra cost; ask our team on WhatsApp.
