Overview

Unwind after a long day in our water lounge. Our spacious double-decker catamaran is the perfect place to enjoy your dinner. Our onboard chef will delight everyone with dishes from international cuisines and his own signature creations.

You will embark on a journey along Dubai's coastline in a luxury floating restaurant, bewitched by the evening lights of the captivating metropolis.

The ship will pass by iconic landmarks such as Dubai Marina - one of the city's most upscale districts, Ain Dubai - the largest Ferris wheel in the world, and the JBR promenade - the city's most popular resort and residential complex.

Brighten up your evening with unique experiences and create memorable photos to reminisce over.

Questions

About this experience

Departure Time?

Boarding starts at 8:30 PM. Departure is at 9:00 PM.

Dress code?

The dress code is smart casual. During the winter season, it's advisable to have warm clothes on hand.

Celebrating a special event?

When booking a cruise, it's important to notify them if there's someone celebrating a birthday. During dinner, dessert will be served at the table accompanied by celebratory music. For an additional charge, you can order a cake. Please contact our representative to place your order.

Child Policy?

Children under the age of 5 for free In case if sharing basis transportation is needed, kids below 5 can go free although  a separate seat will not be provided for them in the bus. A child's ticket is for children between the ages of 5 and 12. A full adult ticket is required from the age of 12.

Is it possible to reserve a specific table in advance?

You won't be able to reserve a specific table, but you can book an upper or lower deck where you'll feel most comfortable.
