Overview

We invite you to get away from the noisy metropolis to the amazing, alluring desert, where the crests of picturesque dunes can be seen in the gentle rays of the velvet sunset.

Specially equipped jeeps with roaring engines will drive you through waves of huge sand dunes into the heart of the desert, where the serene spirit of golden sands reigns.

After a thrilling jeep ride we will find ourselves in a comfortable premium desert camp where you will have dinner and a whole host of live performances.

The noble safari camp is considered one of the best desert camps in the UAE with a #1 or #2 spot in the TripAdvisor rankings. The camp is known for its unique decor, a huge entertainment program featuring oud players, singers, multiple belly dancers, fire show and much more.

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

The form of clothing is comfortable, sporty. Shoes: sports (sneakers, sneakers) or flip-flops. In winter, it is desirable to have warm clothes.

Child Policy?

Children under 3 years old free of charge (no boarding place on the group tour).  Children 3-12 years old - a children's ticket.  Ages 12 and up - adult ticket.

Can I drive an off-road vehicle?

Only licensed tour drivers are allowed to drive an off-road cars. However ATVs and Buggies are available and permitted for visitors to drive.

ATV rides during the safari?

There will be an opportunity to ride an ATV or buggy during the technical stop for an additional fee.  An ATV or buggy ride can be booked in advance with a company representative.

Terms

The price includes:

  • Riding on the dunes,
  • Photo stop at sunset,
  • Riding on camels,
  • Dinner,
  • Show program (belly dancing, Tanoura, Arabic music).

Riding on the dunes is not recommended for persons with medical conditions, pregnant women and young children.

For an additional fee, you can:

  • Order alcoholic drinks and hookah,
  • Rent an ATV or a buggy.
