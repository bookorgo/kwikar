Overview

Burj Khalifa is the tallest building in the world at 828 metres and the main symbol of Dubai. Its silhouette, inspired by the hymenocallis flower, combines bold engineering with motifs of traditional Islamic architecture.

Seen from above, the city opens up anew: by day, a scattering of skyscrapers and the line of the coast; at sunset, the gold of the desert giving way to the evening lights of the metropolis. Three levels of observation decks let you choose the scale of the experience — from the classic panorama to an exclusive lounge above the clouds.

What Awaits You

  • Silver, levels 124 and 125: observation decks at a height of 456 metres with the finest panoramas of the city.
  • Gold, level 148: an observation deck one level higher, with breathtaking views.
  • Platinum, levels 152–154: the highest lounge in the world at a height of 585 metres, with a cup of coffee or tea.

Ideal For

  • A first acquaintance with Dubai: the key stop on the itinerary of every visitor to the emirate.
  • Photography lovers: sunrise, sunset or the evening illuminations of the city from a bird’s-eye view.
  • Special occasions: a cup of coffee or tea in the world’s highest lounge on the Platinum level.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

The dress code is casual; clothing must not be see-through.

What are the conditions for children?

Children under 3 go free. From 3 to 7 years inclusive — child ticket; from 8 years — adult ticket. Proof of the child’s age may be requested at the check-in desk. Pushchairs are not permitted in the lift and remain at the base of the tower until the end of the visit.

How long does the visit take?

The visit usually takes from 1.5 to 2 hours, depending on the number of visitors.

How do I find the check-in desk?

The Burj Khalifa check-in desk is located on the LG (Lower Ground) level of Dubai Mall — follow the signs for “At the Top”.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No, both printed and electronic tickets are accepted.

What documents are required for entry?

Please bring photo identification and your ticket (printed or electronic).

What should I do if I am late?

Speak to the staff at the check-in desk — the Burj Khalifa management will decide whether you may be admitted. If entry is refused, no refund is provided and a new ticket must be purchased.

May I bring my own food and drinks?

No, food and drinks may not be taken onto the observation deck.
