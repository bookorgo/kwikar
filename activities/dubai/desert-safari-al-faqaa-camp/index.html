Overview

Enjoy an unforgettable evening in the Arabian desert with a classic desert safari experience. Leave the city behind and travel into the golden dunes for an exciting off-road adventure, beautiful sunset views, and a relaxing evening at a traditional desert camp.

Your journey begins with pickup from your hotel, followed by a thrilling dune drive across the desert in a comfortable 4x4 vehicle. Along the way, you will have time to stop for photos and take in the peaceful beauty of the desert landscape. Guests may also get the chance to try sandboarding on the dunes.

After the desert drive, continue to a welcoming safari camp where you can relax in a traditional setting with cushions, carpets, and a central stage. Enjoy a short camel ride, try henna painting, take photos in local-style dress, or simply unwind as the evening atmosphere builds.

As night falls, a buffet dinner with BBQ and soft drinks will be served. The evening continues with live entertainment, which may include Tanoura dance, belly dance, and a fire show. This experience is a perfect mix of adventure, culture, dining, and entertainment, making it one of the most popular activities for visitors and residents in the UAE.

Questions

About this experience

What should I wear for the desert safari?

Comfortable casual or sportswear is recommended. Guests may wear sports shoes, sandals, or flip-flops. During the winter season, it is advisable to bring a light jacket or warm clothing, as the desert can become cool in the evening.

What is the child policy?

Children below 2 years old are not permitted on this tour. Children from 2 to 12 years old are usually eligible for a child rate, while guests aged 12 years and above are considered adults. Young children may not be suitable for dune bashing due to the nature of the activity.

Can I drive the 4x4 vehicle myself?

No, guests are not allowed to drive the safari 4x4 vehicle. The dune drive is conducted by experienced drivers. Guests who want a self-drive desert experience may choose to rent a buggy, subject to availability and extra charges.

Can I rent a quad bike or buggy during the tour?

Yes, quad bikes and buggies may be available for rent during the stop at the desert meeting point. These activities are optional and are charged separately.

Is hotel drop-off included?

Yes, return transportation is included. After the dinner and live entertainment, guests will be dropped back to their hotel or designated pickup location.

Why are some desert safari options cheaper?

Desert safari experiences can vary depending on the supplier, camp quality, dune location, vehicle standards, food, entertainment, and overall service level. Cheaper options may use different desert areas or offer a more basic experience. A quality safari usually includes licensed drivers, proper safety standards, a comfortable camp, good food, and reliable service.

Is dune bashing suitable for everyone?

Dune bashing is not recommended for guests with medical conditions, back or neck problems, heart issues, or for very young children. Pregnant women are not allowed to participate in this tour due to safety restrictions.

What is included in the desert safari?

The experience usually includes dune bashing, a photo stop in the desert, a short camel ride, buffet dinner with soft drinks, and live entertainment such as belly dance, Tanoura dance, and fire show.

Are there any optional extras?

Yes, optional extras may include alcoholic drinks, shisha, ATV or buggy rental, and souvenirs. These are not included in the standard package and can be purchased separately.
