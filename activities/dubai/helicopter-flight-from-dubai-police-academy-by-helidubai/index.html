Overview

Dubai is a metropolis whose outline changes depending on where you view it from: on the ground, the city impresses with its sheer scale, while from above it reveals itself in a completely different way — as a single composition of glass, sand and sea.

A few minutes in the air, and familiar landmarks come together into one seamless panorama: a city of the future set against desert and ocean, seen the way few people ever get to see it.

What to Expect

  • Flight over the Dubai skyline: take to the skies in a pilot-flown helicopter and enjoy a bird's-eye view of the metropolis.
  • Iconic landmarks: fly over Burj Khalifa, Burj Al Arab, Palm Jumeirah and The World Islands.
  • Contrasting landscapes: golden desert sands and the azure waters of the Persian Gulf in a single flight.
  • Memorable shots: a chance to photograph the city from the air against its most recognisable views.
  • Choice of routes: flights range from 12 to 40 minutes, with different landmarks depending on the option selected.
  • Ideal For
  • Romantic couples: a special occasion and an unforgettable experience for two over the city in the evening.
  • First-time visitors to Dubai: see the emirate's top attractions from a completely new angle in one short flight.
  • Photography lovers: unique shots of the city and coastline from a bird's-eye view.

Questions

About this experience

What should I wear?

The dress code is casual. Hats and scarves may not be worn during the flight.

How long is the flight?

The duration depends on the route selected — from 12 to 40 minutes. You need to arrive at the helipad 45 minutes before departure for check-in and the safety briefing.

What documents do I need for the flight?

Each passenger must present an original passport or Emirates ID (copies and photos are not accepted), as well as a printed or electronic ticket.

What can I take on board?

Only a phone, camera and sunglasses. Bags, hats and scarves must be left in the storage area.

What happens in adverse weather?

Flights may operate in reduced visibility. If a flight is cancelled due to weather conditions, you will be offered a new date or a refund.

Are there any weight restrictions?

Yes. On shared flights, the weight limit is 110 kg per passenger; passengers over this limit must purchase an additional seat, with an absolute maximum of 140 kg. On private flights, the total limit on board is 400 kg. Seatbelt extenders are not permitted.

Can pregnant women take the flight?

Yes, up to the 32nd week of pregnancy. We recommend getting your doctor's approval beforehand.

What are the rules for children?

Children under 2 are not permitted to fly. From the age of 2, a child requires their own seat and pays the full adult fare. Children aged 2 to 12 inclusive must be accompanied on the flight by an adult over 18.

What are the peculiarities of the tour?

Before the flight is required to pass registration, weighing and briefing.  It is forbidden to use flash photography during the flight.

What is the helicopter's flight altitude?

Flight altitude 270-300 meters.
