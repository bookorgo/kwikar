Overview

Dune Bashing

Experience a real Arabian adventure on a desert safari tour. 

Get on a breathtaking ride in the desert, crossing dunes in a comfortable off-road vehicle, like a real world roller coaster - informally known as 'dune bashing'.

During the safari you will have an opportunity to take stunning pictures of the unmistakable desert beauty as well as try out sand boarding in the steep red dunes.

Desert Camp

After the madness of the dune bashing you will arrive at a desert camp where you will be offered comfortable pillows & cushions to sit on with a stage in the middle of the camp.

At the camp you will be served a full course buffet dinner with live bbq as well as a bar and sheesha station available for extra charge.

The cherry on the cake will be a 3 set live entertainment program consisting of a tanura dance show, belly dance show and a fire show.

Additionally, you will be able to dress in local attire for some exciting photo opportunities and also get 'henna' temporary tatoos that are very common amongst women in the gulf.

The desert safari is a must try tour in the UAE for residents and visitors alike.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

Comfortable sport wear is recommended. Sport shoes or flip-flops. During winter time, it is recommended to bring warm clothes as it may get cold during the evening.

What is the child policy?

Children under 2 years old are not allowed. Children 2-12 years old - a children's rate applicable.  Ages 12 and up are considered adults.

Can I drive a jeep myself?

It is not allowed to drive an off-road vehicle by law. If you wish to go for a self drive option, you have to rent a buggy.

Can I rent a quadbike or a buggy during the tour?

There will be an opportunity to rent a quad bike or buggy during the initial stop at the meeting point for an additional fee.

Is drop off transportation included in the price?

Yes, you will be dropped back to the hotel after the dinner. 

Why are there cheaper options advertised?

'Desert Safari' is a generic term like 'Restaurant' or 'Hotel'.  There are many different suppliers with varying levels of quality. We work with only fully legal suppliers and trusted, quality providers. We do not cut corners on any aspect of the tour. The dune bashing is done in the elite 'red sands' of Sharjah, near the 'Big Red Dunes'. Many suppliers do the dune bashing in a much closer and less picturesque part of the desert, just outside the city. All drivers are experienced, qualified and licensed by DTCM. At the camp, we restrict number of guests to be within a comfortable density. We pay attention to the food quality at the camp as well as employ talented performers to make your evening great. Whenever something is very cheap, there is a catch.
