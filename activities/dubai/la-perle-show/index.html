Overview

La Perle - an exciting blend of artistic performance, visual fantasies, and cutting-edge technology. The director and creator of this 90-minute show is one of the world's most famous directors, Franco Dragone.

You will discover the story of a girl searching for a lost pearl. The show features 65 artists from 23 countries showcasing an incredible range of talents and skills, from dance and acrobatics to water and aerial tricks. 

Acrobats, bikers, and motorcycles defy gravity on stage. Professionals dive from a height of 25 meters into a pool and perform other incredible stunts. Don't miss your chance to experience the thrilling La Perle show.

Questions

About this experience

Dress code?

The dress code is smart casual.

Duration of the show?

The performance lasts for 90 minutes.

Can you bring water and food?

You can bring a 0.5L bottle of water, but outside food is not allowed. There are cafes in the foyer where you can purchase drinks and snacks. You can also order dinner at one of the restaurants in the Habtoor City complex before or after the show (drinks for dinner are paid separately). Please contact our representative to place an order.

Child policy?

Children under 2 years old are not allowed. From 2 years old, adult ticket is required. Documents verifying the child's age may be required at the entrance.

Booking with KWIKAR

How do I book this with KWIKAR?

Tap Check availability, choose your date, guests and option, and send the request on WhatsApp. Our team checks availability with the supplier and replies with the final AED price before you pay.

Are there booking or card fees?

No. The price our team confirms on WhatsApp is the total you pay. KWIKAR does not add a separate booking or card fee.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Changes and refunds follow the supplier's rules for this experience, shown in the cancellation policy on this page. Contact us as soon as possible and we will check what applies to your booking.
