Overview

Palm Jumeirah is a man-made island shaped like a palm tree and one of Dubai's boldest engineering projects. This is where one of the most spectacular ways to see the city begins — from a bird's-eye view.

Within minutes, the scenery transforms: glittering skyscrapers stand alongside golden desert sands and the turquoise expanse of the Persian Gulf. The panorama unfolds quickly and dramatically, leaving you with the feeling of flying over two different worlds at once.

What to Expect

  • Take-off from Palm Jumeirah: lift off from the helipad at Atlantis The Palm and begin your flight over one of the world's most recognisable islands.
  • Burj Khalifa and Burj Al Arab: Dubai's iconic silhouettes from a bird's-eye perspective you simply can't get from the ground.
  • The World Islands: fly over a man-made archipelago shaped like a map of the world (on routes of 15 minutes or longer).
  • The coastline and Dubai Marina: views of Jumeirah Beach, Aquaventure Waterpark and the Marina skyscrapers (on extended routes).
  • Memorable shots: stunning photos and videos against the glittering skyline and turquoise waters of the Gulf.
  • Ideal For
  • Special occasions: special birthday and marriage proposal packages are available.
  • First-time visitors to Dubai: a chance to see all of the city's top attractions in one short flight.
  • Thrill-seekers and photography lovers: a unique perspective and an adrenaline rush you can't get on the ground.

Itinerary

Check-in at the helipad

Arrive 45 minutes before departure at Atlantis The Palm (Palm Jumeirah); transfer to the helipad is not included.

Safety briefing

A short pre-flight briefing, followed by boarding.

Flight over Dubai

12 to 60 minutes depending on the option selected, passing over Burj Al Arab, Palm Jumeirah, Burj Khalifa and other iconic landmarks.

Questions

About this experience

What should I wear?

The dress code is casual; hats and scarves may not be worn during the flight.

Where is the helipad located?

Flights depart from the helipad on Palm Jumeirah, next to Atlantis The Palm.

How early should I arrive before departure?

Check-in opens 45 minutes before departure — please arrive in good time to complete the safety briefing. Late arrivals may be denied boarding without a refund.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No, both printed and electronic tickets are accepted.

Can I bring food and drinks?

No, food and drinks are not allowed on board.

What can I take on board?

You may bring only your smartphone, camera and sunglasses — bags, hats and scarves must be left in the storage area before the flight.

What happens in bad weather?

Flights may operate in reduced visibility. If a flight is cancelled due to weather conditions, you will be offered a new date or a refund.

Can pregnant women take the flight?

Yes, up to the 32nd week of pregnancy; we recommend getting your doctor's approval beforehand.

Are there any weight restrictions?

Yes: on shared flights, the limit is 110 kg per passenger, and passengers over this limit must purchase an additional seat. On private flights, the total limit is 450 kg; seatbelt extenders are not permitted.

What are the rules for children?

Children under 2 are not permitted to fly. From the age of 2, a child requires their own seat and pays the full adult fare; children aged 2–12 must be accompanied by an adult (18+).

What types of tickets are available?

Flights are priced by duration. Available options are 12, 15, 17 and 25 minute flights. The longer flights will include more sights.

Can tickets be refunded or changed?

Tickets are fully refundable 7 days or more prior to the flight, and 50% refundable 4 days or more prior to the flight. No Refunds can be provided for cancellations within 3 days of the booked flight. Flights can be rescheduled up to 24 hours free of charge.

Do I need any special preparations before my flight?

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and shoes. Sunglasses are advisable for daytime flights. Otherwise, just bring your excitement and camera to capture the views!

Are there Age restrictions?

Children 2 years old or younger are not allowed to fly.

Can people of determination fly?

Yes! As long as passenger can sit in the seat in a regular seated position, then they are welcome to fly.

Is Photography allowed?

Guests are welcome to take photos once the flight has begun, but photos are not allowed prior to boarding the aircraft.
