Overview

Experience Highlights

  • ​Soar high above the sparkling Arabian Gulf on a jet-powered Fly board.
  • ​Enjoy stunning, panoramic views of the iconic Dubai skyline.
  • ​Capture incredible photos and videos against the backdrop of the luxurious Burj Al Arab.

​What to Expect

You'll start with a brief safety briefing and get fitted with the necessary gear. Our professional instructors will guide you every step of the way, ensuring you feel confident and ready to fly. You'll quickly learn how to balance and control your height, from gentle hovering to exhilarating flights above the water.

​Perfect for

  • Thrill-seekers.
  • Adventure enthusiasts, and anyone looking to try a unique water sport and see Dubai from a completely new angle.
  • No prior experience is necessary.

Questions

About this experience

What should I bring for water sport session?

You only need your ID or copy and swimwear.

Do I need any experience to ride?

No experience is required. Our instructors guide you step by step to ensure a safe and enjoyable ride.

How early should I arrive before my booking?

We recommend arriving 15–20 minutes earlier to complete registration and receive safety briefing.

Is photo and video included?

Your instructor can make photo and video for you during the ride.

Age policy

The minimum age for Fly board session is 8 years.

Safety precautions

All equipment is maintained to the highest marine standards, and every session is supervised by certified professionals.
