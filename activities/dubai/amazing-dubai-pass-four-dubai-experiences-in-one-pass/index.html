Overview

The Amazing Dubai Pass brings together some of Dubai’s most popular theme parks, city attractions and entertainment experiences in one simple pass: you choose four experiences and plan your own days around them.

Pick two theme parks, one city attraction and one Flex Experience, and visit them in any order within the validity period.

What awaits you

  • Theme parks (choose 2): MOTIONGATE™ Dubai, Real Madrid World, LEGOLAND® Dubai, LEGOLAND® Water Park and Oasis Bay (coming soon).
  • City attractions (choose 1): The Green Planet, The View at The Palm or Ain Dubai (coming soon).
  • Flex Experience (choose 1): Global Village or the Riverland™ Dubai Standard Package - Riverland™ Standard Package: full-day entry to Riverland, Axe Throwing (18+, 5 throws), Archery (10+, 5 arrows), Perplex City (15 minutes), Neon Galaxy (1 hour) and Leisure Cycling (1 hour).

Perfect for

  • Families with children: theme parks, a water park and family entertainment in one pass; children under 3 enter free of charge.
  • First-time visitors to Dubai: a ready-made mix of theme parks and city attractions without buying separate tickets.
  • Travellers who like to plan their own time: 10 days to visit the attractions in any order you like.

Questions

About this experience

What is included in the Amazing Dubai Pass?

Four experiences: two theme parks, one city attraction and one Flex Experience, selected from the lists above.

How long is the pass valid?

The pass is valid for 6 months from the booking date. Once you redeem it for the first time, it is valid for 10 consecutive days, and the attractions can be visited in any order. Any unused admission expires automatically after this period, with no partial refund.

Can I visit the same attraction twice or share the pass?

No. The pass is non-transferable and its inclusions form one package — they may not be separated or sold individually.

What is included in the Riverland™ Standard Package?

Full-day entry to Riverland, Axe Throwing (18+, 5 throws), Archery (10+, 5 arrows), Perplex City (15 minutes), Neon Galaxy (1 hour) and Leisure Cycling (1 hour). Guests who select this package must redeem it at the Perplex City counter before accessing the other included Riverland experiences.

When can I visit The View at The Palm?

Admission to The View at The Palm is valid during non-prime hours for Level 52 only.

Are there age restrictions?

Some Riverland activities have age limits: Axe Throwing is for guests aged 18 and over, and Archery for guests aged 10 and over.

What are the conditions for children?

Children under 3 enter free of charge. Valid proof of age may be required.

Are all attractions available every day?

Blackout dates, operating hours, capacity restrictions and attraction availability may apply. Certain rides, attractions or experiences may be temporarily unavailable due to weather, maintenance or operational requirements, and no full or partial refund is provided in such cases.
