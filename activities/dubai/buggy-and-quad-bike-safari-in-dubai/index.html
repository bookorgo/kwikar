Overview

Experience the thrill of adventure with a memorable buggy and quad tour in Dubai’s stunning desert landscapes. Starting from Badayer, renowned worldwide for its striking red sands, this tour promises an exhilarating journey through some of the most beautiful and expansive desert scenery in the world.

These big dunes provide the perfect playground for thrill-seekers eager to conquer the sands. As you venture into the desert, you'll be greeted by the breathtaking view of endless golden waves, creating a picture-perfect backdrop for an adventure-filled day.

The tour offers a self-drive experience, allowing you to take control of powerful and comfortable vehicles.

You can choose from one, two and four seater buggies. These robust vehicles are designed to handle the challenging terrain with ease, providing a safe yet exciting ride as you navigate the dunes. The buggies are perfect for those who want to feel the adrenaline rush of driving through the desert in their own hands.

Quad bikes are also available, with Yamaha 350cc models that are easy to handle and perfect for solo riders or those who want to experience the desert on their own. Quad biking adds a different dimension to the adventure, offering agility and a closer connection to the terrain.

Throughout the tour, you'll be guided by an experienced professional who ensures your safety and enhances your experience. The guide will take you through the beautiful desert terrain, sharing safety tips to make your ride enjoyable and secure. The guide's expertise allows you to focus on the thrill of the ride while ensuring you navigate the dunes confidently.

This desert tour is more than just an adrenaline rush; it’s a chance to connect with nature's raw beauty. The vibrant red sands, endless dunes, and clear desert skies create a setting that’s both awe-inspiring and peaceful. Whether you're a thrill-seeker or someone seeking a scenic escape, this buggy and quad tour provides an unmatched adventure experience in Dubai’s desert.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

Comfortable sportswear is recommended. Sport shoes or flip-flops. Warm clothes are recommended during winter.

What is the child policy?

Children under 2 years old are not allowed. Children aged 2-12 have a children's rate. Ages 12 and up are considered adults.

Can I drive the buggy myself?

Yes, our buggies are designed for self-driving. Our expert guides will provide guidance and safety protocols before you start. For buggy - 16 years and older can drive. 5-15 can ride as passengers if the driver is a parent. For quad bike - 12 years and older can drive.

Is this activity insured?

No. All buggy/quad operators operate on the same principle. You will be signing a waiver that will waive all liability for the damage to the buggy or quad as well as any personal injury.
