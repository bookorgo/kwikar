Dubai Miracle Garden Tickets

Al Barsha South 3, Dubai · Seasonal flower garden · Open-dated tickets

Season 15: 8 Oct 2026 – 31 May 2027Open-dated ticketBook on WhatsApp

Dubai Miracle Garden is a seasonal outdoor flower garden in Al Barsha South 3, known for its full-size Emirates A380 covered in flowers. Season 15 runs from 8 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, and the garden is open every day from 9am to 9pm (last entry 8:30pm). KWIKAR offers an open-dated adult ticket from AED 105, or a combo with the Dubai Butterfly Garden next door. Children under 3 enter free.

Overview

Dubai Miracle Garden is an outdoor garden built entirely around flowers. The garden says each season brings more than 200 million flowers of over 150 varieties, laid out across 72,000 square metres in more than 50 displays. Because it is outdoors in the desert, it only opens in the cooler months: the garden is replanted every summer, and Season 15 runs from 8 October 2026 to 31 May 2027 under the theme "Fifteen Years, Blooming Together".

The best-known display is a full-size Emirates A380 covered in flowers and living plants, which set a Guinness World Records title in 2016. Other favourites are the floral castle, the Heart Tunnel, the Umbrella Passage, the Floating Lady, a giant teddy bear and a working floral clock more than 15 metres wide. There are shows and buskers on the Big Stage, while extras such as the mini golf, mini train and buggy tour are paid on site.

The Dubai Butterfly Garden is right next door with its own ticket, and KWIKAR offers a combo that covers both. Our team checks your request and confirms the final price before you pay. For more seasonal evenings, see Global Village and Dubai Garden Glow.

Best for

  • Photos: Bright floral displays everywhere, best in the hour before sunset.
  • Families: Easy, flat walking, and children under 3 enter free.
  • Winter visitors: Open October to May, when it is cool enough to stay outdoors.
  • A short outing: Most visitors spend about two hours here.
KWIKAR tip

Weekday mornings are the quietest. For photos, arrive about an hour before sunset and stay to see the garden lit up after dark. If you have the combo, see the Butterfly Garden first, as it closes at 6pm.

Visitor information

Opening hours
Open every day of Season 15 (8 October 2026 to 31 May 2027) from 9am to 9pm, with last entry at 8:30pm. Hours can change on public holidays and event days; our team confirms the hours for your date.
Children
Children under 3 enter free; from age 3 a child ticket is needed. Proof of age, such as an Emirates ID, may be requested at the entrance. Send the ages of your children and our team confirms the right tickets.
Getting there
The garden is in Al Barsha South 3 and the main entrance is Gate 1. By public transport, take the Metro Red Line to Mall of the Emirates, then RTA bus 105 (seasonal, so check RTA first) or a taxi of about 10 to 15 minutes. We can also add a private transfer.
Not allowed
Outside food and drink, barbecues, pets, bicycles, scooters, hoverboards and balls are not allowed. Smoking is not allowed anywhere in the garden, and picking flowers is strictly forbidden.
Cameras
Phone and personal photos are fine. Professional cameras and drones are not allowed, and event or wedding shoots need permission from the garden's management first.
Butterfly Garden
Dubai Butterfly Garden is next door and needs its own ticket or the combo. It closes earlier than the Miracle Garden (6pm), so visit the butterflies first.

About this experience

How much are Dubai Miracle Garden tickets?

The adult ticket is from AED 105 per person, and the live price of each option is shown on this page. Our team confirms the final price on WhatsApp before you pay.

Is Dubai Miracle Garden open now?

Season 15 runs from 8 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, and the garden is open every day in that period. It closes in summer and is replanted for the next season.

What are the Dubai Miracle Garden timings?

Every day from 9am to 9pm, with last entry at 8:30pm. Hours can change on public holidays and event days, so our team confirms the time for your date.

Are children free at Dubai Miracle Garden?

Children under 3 enter free. From age 3 a child ticket is needed, and staff may ask for proof of age at the entrance. Send your children's ages and our team confirms the tickets you need.

Is the Butterfly Garden included?

Not with the general ticket. The Dubai Butterfly Garden is next door and has its own entry, but KWIKAR offers a combo that covers both gardens, and you can visit them on different days.

How long does a visit take?

Most visitors spend about two hours. Add time if you plan to visit the Butterfly Garden too or stay for the evening lights.

When is the best time to visit?

Weekday mornings are usually the quietest. For photos, arrive about an hour before sunset and stay as the garden lights up after dark.

How do I get to Dubai Miracle Garden?

It is in Al Barsha South 3. Take the Metro Red Line to Mall of the Emirates, then RTA bus 105 (seasonal) or a short taxi ride. The ticket does not include transport, but we can add a private transfer.

Can I bring food or my dog?

No. Outside food and drink are not allowed, and neither are pets, bicycles, scooters, hoverboards or balls. There are places to eat inside the garden.

Can I use a drone or professional camera?

No. Professional cameras and drones are not allowed. Photos on your phone or a personal camera are fine, but please do not pick the flowers.
