Alexandra New Year's Eve Gala Dinner Cruise in Dubai

Thursday 31 December 2026 · 10pm to 1am · Buffet, live shows and midnight fireworks

31 December onlyFireworks from the waterBook on WhatsApp

The Alexandra New Year's Eve gala dinner is a three-hour cruise on Thursday 31 December 2026: the boat leaves at 10pm, you have a full-course buffet and live entertainment on board, watch the midnight fireworks from the water and are back at about 1am. You choose the boat (the Alexandra 2 dhow or the Alexandra Sea Lounge), a table on the upper deck or the lower deck, and whether to meet at the boat or add a shared hotel transfer. Overview

Alexandra runs dinner cruises on the water in Dubai all year, and on New Year's Eve two of its boats become gala venues: the Alexandra 2 dhow, a traditional wooden boat, and the Alexandra Sea Lounge. Both sail for three hours, from 10pm until about 1am, so you are on the water when the clock strikes midnight.

The evening starts with live entertainment: a tanura dancer in a spinning, lit-up skirt and a magic show, with a live singer on the Sea Lounge. Dinner is a full-course buffet with a special New Year's Eve menu, served with soft drinks and water. Alcoholic drinks can be bought on board by guests aged 21 and over.

At midnight the boat is out on the water for the fireworks. The operator's New Year's Eve plan describes a cruise into the Business Bay canal for the Burj Khalifa display, while the regular Alexandra cruises sail in Dubai Marina, so ask our team which boat and viewing spot apply to your booking if the Burj Khalifa view matters most. To watch the fireworks right next to the tower instead, compare New Year's Eve dinner at Sky Views Observatory, or read our guide to dhow cruise vs yacht.

Best for

  • Couples: A table on the upper deck for dinner and the countdown on the water.
  • Families: Soft drinks, a buffet and child prices on every option.
  • Groups of friends: A three-hour party with shows, with drinks to buy on board.
KWIKAR tip

Choose a sharing transfer option or book a private car in advance: taxis are hard to find in Dubai on New Year's Eve, and the boat leaves at 10pm sharp.

Which should I choose?

  • Open-air views: Upper deck: an open deck with the clearest view of the skyline and the fireworks. Costs more than the lower deck.
  • Lower price, sheltered seat: Lower deck: the same dinner and shows at a table on the lower deck.
  • Traditional wooden dhow: Alexandra 2 dhow: boarding from 9pm, tanura and magic shows.
  • A live singer too: Alexandra Sea Lounge: boarding from 9:30pm, tanura, magic show and a live singer.
  • No taxi on the busiest night: Sharing transfer options: a shared vehicle collects you from your Dubai hotel in the evening and brings you back after the cruise.

Visitor information

Where to board
Both boats leave from a marina in Dubai. Our team sends the exact berth and boarding instructions with your confirmation, because the boarding point for New Year's Eve can differ from the everyday cruise.
Arrive early
Roads across Dubai are busy and some close on New Year's Eve, and the boat cannot wait for late guests. Leave plenty of time, or choose a sharing transfer option or a private car so you do not have to look for a taxi.
Transfers
On the sharing transfer options, pickup is between about 7pm and 9pm depending on your area; the exact time is confirmed closer to the date. The shared vehicle brings you back after the cruise.
Upper or lower deck
Both decks get the same buffet and shows. The upper deck is open to the air, so bring a light layer for a December night on the water.
Drinks and age
Soft drinks and water are included. Alcohol is sold on board only to guests aged 21 and over, and ID can be requested. Outside food and drinks are not allowed.
Children
Every option has a child price, but age rules for this night can differ between the boats. Send the ages of the children in your group and our team confirms what applies before you pay.

About this experience

When is the Alexandra New Year's Eve cruise?

On Thursday 31 December 2026. The boat departs at 10pm and returns at about 1am. Boarding starts at 9pm on the Alexandra 2 dhow and at 9:30pm on the Alexandra Sea Lounge.

How much is the Alexandra New Year's Eve gala dinner?

The price depends on the boat, the deck and whether you add a transfer: the lower deck without a transfer costs the least, and the Sea Lounge upper deck with a sharing transfer the most. Our team confirms the final price before you pay.

Can you see the Burj Khalifa fireworks from the cruise?

The operator's New Year's Eve plan describes a cruise into the Business Bay canal for the Burj Khalifa fireworks. Routes on the night can change, so if the Burj Khalifa view is a must, ask our team which boat and viewing spot apply before you book.

What is included?

A three-hour cruise, a table on your deck, a full-course buffet with a special New Year's Eve menu, soft drinks and water, a tanura dance and magic show (plus a live singer on the Sea Lounge), and the fireworks from the water.

Are alcoholic drinks included?

No. Alcohol is sold on board at extra cost, only to guests aged 21 and over.

What is the difference between the upper and lower deck?

Both decks get the same dinner and shows. The upper deck is open-air, with clearer views of the skyline and the fireworks, and costs more.

Is hotel pickup included?

Only on the sharing transfer options, which collect you from your Dubai hotel between about 7pm and 9pm and bring you back after the cruise. On the other options you meet the boat at the berth.

Can children join the New Year's Eve cruise?

Every option has a child price, but age rules for this night can differ between the boats. Send the children's ages and our team confirms what applies.

What should I wear?

Smart casual. Bring a light jacket or shawl, especially for the upper deck, because nights on the water in December can feel cool.
