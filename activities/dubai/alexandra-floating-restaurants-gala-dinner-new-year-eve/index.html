How it works

  1. Boarding Starts

    Guests will be greeted at the Marina By Our Crew and directed to board the boat.

  2. Boat Departs

    Once all of the guests have Boarded, the boat will set sail into the Business Bay Canal

  3. Live Entertainment

    Once the boat has set sail, guests will be treated to a live entertainment Tanura Light show.

  4. Buffet Dinner

    Guests will be treated to a full course premium buffet dinner.

  5. Burj Khalifa Fireworks Display

    The Center Piece of the NYE Gala Cruise and Dubai's NYE celebration: Fireworks in the Burj Khalifa

  6. Return to Marina

    Guests will be brought back to the Marina
