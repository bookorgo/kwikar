Overview

Dinner in the Sky in Dubai is a unique gastronomic experience, characterized not only by exquisite dishes but also by its exceptional setting. It takes place on a specially suspended platform, located 50 meters above the ground, equipped with everything necessary for an enjoyable and safe experience.


Dinner in the Sky - is the most panoramic restaurant in the city, where there are no barriers between you and the breathtaking views of the sparkling azure waters of the bay and the metropolis, with its towering facades of modern architectural giants.


The restaurant "Dinner in the Sky" has rightfully earned its reputation as one of the most unique establishments. It has been listed in the top 10 of Forbes magazine as a recommended destination for those who love new experiences.

Questions

About this experience

How long does dinner takes?

The entire event lasts approximately 90 minutes: 15 minutes for boarding and ascent, 60 minutes for dinner, and 15 minutes for descent.

Dress code?

The dress code is smart casual, and it is recommended to wear warm clothing as there may be wind at a height of 50 meters. Closed-toe shoes that fit tight are highly recommended.

Cancellation policy?

The reserved dinner cannot canceled, rescheduled, or transferred to third parties for any other reason but bad weather conditions. In the event of inclement weather, the waiting time for the ascent can be up to 90 minutes. If the conditions do not normalize within this time, the dinner will be rescheduled or canceled. In the case of cancellation due to weather conditions, the dinner might be rescesdulled or the payment for the dinner will be fully refunded.

What else should I know?

Guests with a height of at least 110 cm and a weight of no more than 135 kg are invited for dinner. Children aged 14 and under must be accompanied by an adult (18+). Guests with disabilities are required to inform in advance about their condition so that the administration can be prepared for their arrival and ensure their comfortable accommodation. Guests with diabetes or allergies should also inform in advance about their condition. Guests under the influence of alcohol or drugs are not allowed to attend dinner without refunding the payment.
