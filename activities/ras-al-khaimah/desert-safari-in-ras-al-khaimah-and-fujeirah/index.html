Overview

Experience the magic of the Arabian desert with our Evening Safari in Ras Al-Khaimah. This thrilling tour takes you on a journey through the stunning dunes of RAK, where you'll witness the beauty of the desert landscape and immerse yourself in the rich culture of the region. Your adventure begins with a pick-up from your hotel in RAK, where you'll be transported to the desert in a comfortable vehicle. As you arrive at the dunes, get ready for an adrenaline-pumping experience of dune bashing. Hold on tight as your skilled driver navigates the sandy terrain, taking you on a thrilling ride over the golden dunes. After the exhilarating dune bashing, you'll have the opportunity to capture the breathtaking sunset views with a photo stop. Watch as the sun paints the sky in vibrant hues, creating a stunning backdrop for your pictures. Next, you'll be taken to a traditional desert camp, where you can indulge in a range of exciting activities. Take a short camel ride and experience the gentle sway of these magnificent creatures as you explore the desert. Try your hand at sandboarding, a thrilling sport that allows you to glide down the dunes on a board, or rent an ATV or buggy for an exhilarating ride. As the evening sets in, feast on a delicious BBQ dinner under the starlit sky. Indulge in a variety of local and international dishes, and savor the flavors of the region. While you dine, be entertained by live performances that showcase the vibrant culture of the Arabian desert. From mesmerizing belly dancers to traditional music, you'll be captivated by the enchanting ambiance. After an unforgettable evening, you'll be transferred back to your hotel, with memories that will last a lifetime. Join us for an Evening Safari in Ras Al-Khaimah and experience the beauty and excitement of the Arabian desert.

Questions

About this experience

What is the dress code?

Comfortable sportswear is recommended, along with sport shoes or flip-flops. In winter, warm clothes are advisable for the evening.

What is the child policy?

Children under 2 years old are not allowed. Children aged 2-12 years old have a children's rate, while ages 12 and up are considered adults.

Can I drive the 4x4 car on the dunes myself?

It is not allowed to drive an off-road vehicle by law. If you wish to go for a self-drive option, you can rent a buggy during the tour.

Can I rent a quadbike or a buggy during the tour?

There will be an opportunity to rent a quad bike or buggy during the initial stop at the meeting point for an additional fee.

Is drop off transportation included in the price?

Yes, you will be dropped back to the hotel after the dinner. 

Why are there cheaper options advertised?

'Desert Safari' is a generic term like 'Restaurant' or 'Hotel'.  There are many different suppliers with varying levels of quality. We do not cut corners on any aspect of the tour, and take our guests to the most picturesque parts of the desert that are further away from the city. All drivers are experienced, qualified and licensed to carry tourists. At the camp, we restrict number of guests to be within a comfortable density. We pay attention to the food quality at the camp as well as employ talented performers to make your evening great. Whenever something is very cheap, there is a catch.
