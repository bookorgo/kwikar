Overview

Dubai is the tourism hub of the UAE, where record-breaking shopping malls rise beside opera houses, souqs and the world's tallest tower — a city built on contrast and scale.

For a few hours, the everyday rhythm of another emirate gives way to the buzz of Dubai's biggest names: glass towers overhead, an aquarium behind mall walls, and the choice to explore freely or chart your own course across the city.

What awaits you

  • The Dubai Mall: the world's largest shopping and entertainment center, listed in the Guinness Book of Records, with an aquarium and countless shops.
  • Burj Khalifa and Souq Al Bahar: views of the world's tallest tower and the nearby traditional-style souq and Dubai Opera.
  • Sharing basis: five hours of free time to shop, dine and explore the mall and surrounding landmarks.

Perfect for

  • Guests staying in other emirates: an easy day trip to Dubai without arranging your own transport.
  • Shoppers and families: free time at the world's largest mall, with shops, an aquarium and dining all in one place.

About this experience

What is the recommended dress code?

Smart casual is recommended. Bring something warm as the bus is air-conditioned.

Is a return transfer included?

Yes, a drop-off transfer is included in the price. If you decide to stay longer at the Dubai Mall or leave earlier, please let your guide know in advance.

What are the conditions for visiting with children?

Children under 3 travel free of charge, without a separate seat on group tours. From 3 to 11 years inclusive — child ticket; from 12 years — adult ticket.
