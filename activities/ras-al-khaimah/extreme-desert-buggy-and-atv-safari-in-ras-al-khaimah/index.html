Overview

Local residents love riding on lightweight sports cars - buggies with reinforced frames and roll cages, and they recommend all guests of the Emirates to experience the true thrill of racing with the desert wind over steep sand dunes. You will embark on a desert adventure behind the wheel of a 2-seater or 4-seater desert buggy or a single-seater quad bike. 

Standing on the crest of a silky sand dune, bathed in the rays of the setting sun, you will capture incredibly beautiful photos. 

After the ride, you will head to a Bedouin camp for dinner. At the camp, a show program awaits you, including belly dancing, Tanoura dance, Arabian music and camel rides.
